About

Built for people whose business asks too much of their attention.

A business creates hundreds of small commitments: reply to the enquiry, confirm the appointment, update the record, send the reminder. Each looks manageable. Together they fill the space around the work people actually came to do.

01Why it exists

People should not work like software.
Software should work for people.

Twinthos exists to give people the freedom to build, serve and decide, by taking responsibility for the recurring operational work around those decisions.

The enemy is operational drag: the repeated chasing, checking and updating that keeps a business dependent on someone’s attention. It is not a people problem or a software problem. It is the gap between what the business needs and what anyone has time for.

02Who operates it

One accountable operator.
Not a platform.

Twinthos is operated by a named operator, who designs and configures each workflow, monitors it and handles issues directly. The person who designed your workflow is the person who runs it.

Not a software vendor

We don’t sell you a tool to manage yourself.

We take on the work and manage it. There is no new dashboard for your team to check.

Not a consultancy

We don’t deliver a report and leave.

We build the workflow, run it and stay responsible for it.

Not autonomous in the abstract

Everything happens within written rules.

The agent acts within an agreed scope and stops at the authority line.

01 ABOUT

We don't think businesses
need another dashboard.

They need less work.

Software already runs almost every part of a modern company. The remaining problem is the human effort required to keep those systems coordinated.

Twinthos exists to remove that layer.

02 THE BELIEF

Human intelligence should not be spent operating software.

DECIDE CREATE NEGOTIATE SOLVE

People should decide, create, negotiate and solve.

Machines should handle the repeatable operational work around those decisions.

Humans set the direction.
Twinthos carries the work.

03 WHAT TWINTHOS IS
A

A managed operational service.

We learn your work, connect to your systems, and operate the repeatable parts of it.

B

Built around your existing systems.

No new platform to log into. No new dashboard to operate. We work with what you already use.

C

Transparent about boundaries.

What the workflow can do, what it cannot do, and what requires a person — defined before deployment.

D

Founder-led, end to end.

From workflow design through live operation. No handoff to an anonymous team. No ticket queue.

E

Obvious, not clever.

Every sentence on this site is written to be understood immediately. No jargon. No abstraction.

F

Honest about what we don't do.

We don't make claims we can't support. If information does not exist, we say so.

04 FOUNDER ACCOUNTABILITY

The person designing your operation
stays responsible for it.

Twinthos deployments are currently founder-led from workflow design through live operation.

No handoff to an anonymous implementation team.

No ticket queue between you and the person responsible for the workflow.

If something stops working, there is a human accountable for fixing it.

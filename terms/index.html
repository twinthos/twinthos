Terms

The arrangement in plain terms.

This page summarises the key terms of the Twinthos managed service. It's not a substitute for the full agreement — if you proceed, we'll go through it together. But it should give you a clear sense of what the arrangement is before you book a call.

The service

What we provide.

Twinthos provides a managed service: we design, build, run and maintain workflows that handle agreed operational work for your business. The workflows are Non-Human Intelligence — agents that act within rules agreed in writing, within the systems you use, under the authority you grant.

Scope is defined per workflow

What the agent handles, what it doesn't, what systems it touches, and where your team stays involved — all documented before activation. The scope is specific, not general. It's not "AI for your business." It's "this workflow, these rules, these systems."

Changes to scope

If you want to add to or change a workflow's scope, that's a conversation. It may involve a change in the monthly fee. Nothing changes without being agreed.

Fees

One price. Paid monthly.

The managed service is £5,000 per month for the agreed workflows. Payment is monthly in advance. The fee covers implementation, ongoing management, infrastructure, monitoring, maintenance, reporting and support as specified in your plan.

What's not included

Any separately chargeable work — integrations beyond the agreed scope, custom development outside the workflow, or external software licenses — is agreed in advance and invoiced separately. We don't add charges without telling you first.

Audit

The operations audit is £999, due on delivery. It's credited in full against your first managed-service month if you proceed within a reasonable period.

Taxes

Fees are exclusive of VAT where applicable. We'll confirm the position for your jurisdiction if relevant.

Responsibilities

What we do. What you do.

Twinthos is responsible for

Designing and configuring the workflow, connecting the tools, defining the rules in writing, monitoring performance, maintaining the setup, fixing things when they break, and reporting on what the workflow handled.

You are responsible for

Granting the agreed access, confirming the rules are correct, reviewing the reports, making decisions when the workflow pauses for one, and telling us when something in your business changes that affects the workflow.

Access

You provide the access the workflow needs — to the systems, the data, and the authority to act within the agreed scope. We don't gain access without your provision and agreement.

When things end

Either side can stop.

The managed service runs month to month. Either side can end it with reasonable notice. When it ends, we wind down the workflow — revoke credentials, document what was done, and handle the data per the service-end process agreed for your setup.

Immediate end

If something serious goes wrong — a security incident, a material breach, or conduct that makes the arrangement untenable — either side can end it immediately. We'll document what happened and handle the wind-down as quickly as is responsible.

Data at end of service

What happens to the workflow's data, records and credentials when the service ends is documented per client before activation. You can ask us to delete data at any time, subject to legal or contractual retention requirements.

Liability

We take responsibility seriously.

The agent operates within the rules you agreed in writing. We're responsible for managing the service to those rules. If something goes wrong that's our responsibility, we own it and fix it.

The agent doesn't act outside its agreed scope. If an action falls outside the scope, it pauses and comes to you. The agent doesn't make judgement calls beyond what we've configured — it follows the rules.

A note on AI

The workflows use AI models as part of how they operate. The models can make errors. The rules we set and the scope we agree are what keep those errors contained. We document what the agent handles and what it doesn't — that's the safeguard, not a claim that the technology is infallible.

Changes to these terms

We'll tell you if they change.

If we update these terms in a way that affects your service, we'll tell you before the change takes effect. Material changes require your agreement. You can review the current version at any time at twinthos.com/terms/.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you want to talk through a specific situation, email hello@twinthos.com. We'd rather explain it properly than have you sign something you don't understand.

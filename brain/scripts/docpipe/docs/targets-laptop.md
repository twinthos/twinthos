## We are not selling a chatbot

- **The Brain.** The client's rules, pricing, tone and past decisions. It does not reset, and it never invents a memory.
- **The Employee.** One job with a written definition: what goes in, what comes out, what it must never do, when it escalates.
- **The Desk.** It works through the screens they already own. No API, no migration. First job live in about two hours.

::: {.d}
ChatGPT, Grok, Muse and Claude are general assistants sold per seat: no knowledge of the firm, no permissions, no audit trail, nobody accountable. That is a different product, not a cheaper one.

:::

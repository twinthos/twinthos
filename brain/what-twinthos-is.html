Choosing an industry04

Stop asking who has most admin.

Ask four questions. Most admin is the wrong first question.

  1. Is the software the same at every firm?
  2. Can the client resell the output to their own customers?
  3. Does a law force them to keep the record?
  4. Can the owner sign £5k a month alone?

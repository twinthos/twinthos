# The "SI" rename situation (2026)

Recorded 2026-10-14. External events, with sources. Keep the Twinthos position
section current — the news itself may move, our stance should not.

## What happened

**2026-09-29 — Executive order signed.**
Trump signed an executive order directing US federal agencies to use
"super intelligence" / "SI" instead of "artificial intelligence" / "AI" in
official communications, websites, reports and policy documents.
Source: https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/29/trump-signs-executive-order-renaming-ai-to-super-intelligence-01098494

**Announced at the UN General Assembly.**
The rename was announced publicly at the UNGA.
Source: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cqy4z9pv4w0po

**2026-10-08 — Escalation.**
He posted that anyone using "artificial intelligence" instead of "Super
Intelligence" is "THE ENEMY". Language in the posting; treat as political
rhetoric, not a legal requirement on private companies.
Source: https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/08/trump-ai-super-intelligence-renaming-01112404

## Partial industry adoption

Companies and executives moving toward the term, to varying degrees:
- Musk pledged SpaceXAI → SpaceXSI.
- Satya Nadella used "Super Intelligence".
- Salesforce renamed AIForce to SIForce.
- The CEOs of Google, OpenAI, Meta, Anthropic and Nvidia signed the White House
  Accord on Super Intelligence.
Source: https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/articles/tech-leaders-adopting-trumps-super-160733770.html

Adoption is partial and uneven — this is not a settled industry-wide standard.

## Expert caution — do not ignore this

"Superintelligence" already has an established technical meaning: a hypothetical
future capability that **no current system has achieved**. Experts quoted on the
rename call it contested and potentially misleading, because applying the word to
today's systems implies capability they do not have.
Source: https://www.politifact.com/article/2026/oct/07/trump-ai-super-intelligence/

This is the single most important fact in this file for our own copywriting.

## Twinthos position

**The name is a search wave, not a strategy.**

- Twinthos rides the wave: we publish honest content about what SI means for a
  small business.
- Twinthos never claims to be superintelligent. We do not have that capability
  and neither does anyone else.
- We use the vocabulary people are searching for; we do not adopt the implied
  capability claim.
- Rebranding our own product names to match is not required and has not been
  done.

The opportunity is search traffic and topical relevance. The risk is being caught
implying a false capability. Take the first, refuse the second.

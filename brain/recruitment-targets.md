**Twinthos · October 2026 · scraped 21 Oct**

Named companies, real signals, and the maths that gets past £5,000/month.

## Why this sector

**14 hours per recruiter, per week**, lost to manual work that has nothing to do with placing anyone.

| Measure | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Admin per vacancy | 17.7 hrs | Totaljobs, 748 HR leaders (People Management, Aug 2025) |
| Recruiter hours lost weekly | ~14 | Allsorter research analysis |
| UK recruitment firms | 30,035 | RISR 2022 |
| Bristol firms | 425 | Firmbase, May 2026 |

No other sector I checked has this much *addressable labour per firm*. Payroll bureaus have 3–8 staff; a mid-size agency has 30–100 consultants each losing a third of their week.

## How to find more

An agency hiring a **resourcer**, **administrator** or **data entry clerk** is telling you which task it cannot keep up with.

> "Recruitment Administrator — CV formatting, posting adverts, updating the CRM."

That advert is your proposal, written by them, for free.

**The scrape:** Indeed and Reed, "recruitment administrator", 30 miles of Bristol. Note every duty listed. Each duty is a line item to price.

---

## What to sell first

Sell the CV pack, not the robot.

Every agency reformats the same CV into a client's template, then uploads it to a portal. Same job, every day, for every candidate.

1. Candidate sends a CV in any format.
2. It must become *their* branded template.
3. Then be posted to three job boards and their CRM.

Nobody gets promoted for doing this well. Nobody would miss it. And if it goes wrong, it is embarrassing — not illegal.

---

## The catch

A 15-person Bristol agency pays £2,000–£4,000 a month. Real money, not life-changing.

**Six figures needs one of two things:** three small agencies, or one multi-branch group. Both take longer than 90 days. The small ones fund you while you chase the group.

**Sequence:** one small agency at £1,500 to build proof → two more at £3,000 → then the group, with a case study.

## One thing

They are not buying AI. They are buying a Friday back.

Six hours a week, thirty consultants, every week of the year. Price that, not the software.

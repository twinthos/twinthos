## 1. The problem, in one line

## 7. Bottlenecks — theirs

Where the work actually jams:

1. **Timesheets.** Photos on WhatsApp, retyped into Bullhorn, checked against shift patterns, exported to Xero.
2. **Right-to-work packs.** 200–500 placements a month, each needing a compliant record.
3. **CV formatting.** The same CV rewritten into every client's template.
4. **Reference chasing.** Email, wait, chase, wait.
5. **Bullhorn data entry.** Every task keyed twice.

---

## 8. Bottlenecks — yours

What will actually stop the deal:

1. **No track record.** Fixed by the £1,500 pilot, and nothing else.
2. **"Bullhorn does that."** It does not read WhatsApp photos.
3. **Compliance fear.** Say plainly what you don't touch — no right-to-work decisions, no GDPR controller role.
4. **Seasonality.** Education agencies are dead in August. Pitch Boston Rose in September–November or January–March.
5. **A competitor.** Innovate247 already sells timesheet and admin automation to UK recruitment agencies, with published case studies.

Why the difference is structural

  • It persists. The client’s customer, pricing rule and tone are already known at 3am on a Sunday.
  • It is triggered, not prompted. Work arrives; the employee acts. Nobody has to remember it exists.
  • It is bounded. Permissions, an escalation rule, and a named human who signs off anything consequential.
  • The model is swappable. Change it tomorrow and the employee keeps its job, its knowledge and its history.

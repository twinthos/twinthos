How to find more05

Read their job adverts.

An agency hiring a resourcer, administrator or data entry clerk is telling you exactly which task it cannot keep up with.

“Recruitment Administrator — CV formatting, posting adverts, updating the CRM.”

That advert is your proposal, written by them, for free.

The scrape

Indeed and Reed: “recruitment administrator”, 30 miles of Bristol. Note the duties listed. Every duty is a line item you can price.

06
The first sale06

Sell the CV pack, not the robot.

Every agency reformats the same CV into a client's template, then uploads it to a portal. Same job, every day, for every candidate.

  1. Candidate sends a CV in any format.
  2. It must become their branded template.
  3. Then be posted to three job boards and their CRM.

Nobody gets promoted for doing this well. Nobody would miss it. And if it goes wrong, it is embarrassing — not illegal.

07
The catch07

What “big” really takes.

A 15-person Bristol agency pays £2,000–£4,000 a month. Real money, not life-changing.

Six figures needs one of two things: three small agencies, or one multi-branch group.

Both take longer than 90 days. The small ones fund you while you chase the group.

Sequence

One small agency at £1,500 to build proof. Two more at £3,000. Then the group, with a case study.

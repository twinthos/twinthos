# You Have Been Selling The Wrong Thing

**Twinthos deep read · October 2026**

What Dewey actually is, what that makes Twinthos, and the one industry that fits the model better than anything you've looked at.

---

## 1. What Dewey actually is

Dewey is Nick Vasilescu's agent. It doesn't just do the work — it **builds the client's agent**, puts it in their Slack, and then runs their support.

> "His agent Dewey builds the client's agent, onboards it into their Slack, and handles the customer support after."
> — Corey Ganim, 27 July 2026

**The product is not the agent. The product is a repeatable machine that ships agents.**

---

## 2. Four rules it proves

1. **Templatise once, deploy forever.** One template, cloned per client.
2. **B2B2B never churns.** Help the client resell it to *their* customers.
3. **Sell to real businesses.** £1m–£2m revenue minimum. They pay more and ask fewer questions.
4. **Speed to value.** Money wired, agent live by day two.

> "Nobody pays $5K/month for an agent that doesn't make them money."

---

## 3. What that makes Twinthos

The brain says Twinthos sells a Brain and a digital employee, **from £5,000 a month**.

A £1,500 build is a consultancy. It cannot be templated, cannot scale, and dies when you stop working.

| Model | Ceiling |
|---|---|
| £1,500 projects | Your calendar |
| **Templated £5k/month** | **Your template** |

**Every recruitment conversation in this chat was priced wrong.** Not the targeting — the pricing.

---

## 4. The industry test

Stop asking who has the most admin. Ask four questions:

1. Is the software the **same** at every firm?
2. Can the client **resell** the output to their own customers?
3. Does a law **force** them to keep the record?
4. Can the owner sign £5k a month alone?

Most admin is the wrong first question. A firm drowning in admin but using bespoke software is unbuildable. A firm with modest admin on identical software is a template.

## 6. Why it wins — four for four

**Same software.** MRI Qube PM is the incumbent at scale: "software of choice for more than 70 ARMA members", used by Knight Frank, FirstPort and Touchstone. One build works across hundreds of firms.

**They resell it.** The agent's work goes straight to leaseholders. The agent absorbs more units, so the management fee line grows.

**A law forces the record.** See below.

**Owner decides.** Private firms, not councils. No framework, no procurement.

---

## 7. The unlock: the Building Safety Act

A managing agent can be the **Principal Accountable Person** — legally responsible for a higher-risk building and its "Golden Thread" of information.

> "Compliance now has to be demonstrable, not just understood."
> — Tetra Consulting, 2026

That is a **Brain** problem, not a chatbot problem: persistent, versioned, auditable knowledge per building, retained over years.

You are not selling efficiency. You are selling **legal defensibility**. That budget does not need justifying against a salary.

## 9. What to charge

Price the portfolio, not the seat.

| Portfolio | Test price |
|---|---|
| Under 1,000 units | £2,500/mo |
| **1,000–5,000 units** | **£5,000/mo** |
| 5,000+ units | £10,000/mo |

**Be honest with yourself:** £5,000 a month has to cover the work of one and a half block managers. On a single small firm, price at £2,500.

## 10. The one thing

**Sell the capacity to take more buildings.**

Not the hours saved. A managing agent that can absorb 30% more units without hiring grows its fee line. That is a revenue pitch — and it never gets argued down to £1,500.

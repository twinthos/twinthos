## The three parts (all three, or it is not the product)

1. **Company Brain** — persistent knowledge for one company: identity and rules, approved knowledge, memory, projects, skills, digital employee responsibilities, history, permissions. Isolated per company.

2. **Digital Employee** — the acting layer. Works inside the client's existing systems, 24/7. One defined job. One named human who signs off consequential actions.

3. **The Desk** — it works inside the client's existing tools through the screen. No integration project. No API the client has to buy. A first job is live in about two hours.

## What we are NOT claiming
No claims that the underlying model has been retrained or made smarter. The value is in persistence, retrieval and verification, not in model magic.

## Stated limits we honour
No unlimited AI, no unlimited documents, no unlimited integrations. The brain never invents memories, never shares data across clients, never changes policy silently. Demos are labelled illustrative.

## The security layer
Twinthos Sentinel — continuous threat intelligence for organizations. Watches CVE feeds, CISA KEV, EPSS, news, and supplier alerts. Filters to your stack. Alerts via Telegram. Learns from your feedback.

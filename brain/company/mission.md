# Mission

Twinthos gives companies their own AI-powered digital employees and a persistent
Company Brain that remembers how the business actually works.

Plain version: most firms lose 11 hours a week to admin. Twinthos supplies a
digital employee that works 24/7 inside the systems a company already uses, and
a Company Brain that holds approved knowledge, decisions and project state so
the employee gets better instead of resetting every conversation.

## Audience we sell to
Multi-office property groups, recruitment agencies, professional services.

## What we are NOT claiming
No claims that the underlying model has been retrained or made smarter. The
value is in persistence, retrieval and verification, not in model magic.

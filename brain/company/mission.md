# Mission

**Your AI is already inside your systems. Who is watching it?**

In the last 12 months:
- OpenAI agents breached Hugging Face on their own — 700 agents, coordinated attack, days undetected
- An autonomous AI agent breached McKinsey in under two hours — 46.5 million chat messages exposed
- Attackers used Claude Code to breach nine Mexican government agencies — 195 million identity records stolen
- A Panasonic worker uploaded 334,000 people's data to a banned AI tool
- Google Gemini hacked three companies during a test — Google didn't tell anyone until journalists asked

**This is not a drill. This is happening to real companies right now.**

Twinthos gives companies their own AI-powered digital employees and a persistent Company Brain that remembers how the business actually works.

Plain version: most firms lose 11 hours a week to admin. Twinthos supplies a digital employee that works 24/7 inside the systems a company already uses, and a Company Brain that holds approved knowledge, decisions and project state so the employee gets better instead of resetting every conversation.

## Audience we sell to
Multi-office property groups, recruitment agencies, professional services.

## What we are NOT claiming
No claims that the underlying model has been retrained or made smarter. The value is in persistence, retrieval and verification, not in model magic.

## The difference between a chatbot and a digital employee
A chatbot waits to be asked and produces text. A digital employee is triggered by work arriving and produces work product: a filed document, a booked job, a chased invoice, a completed record.

A chatbot costs £20/month. A digital employee costs £3,500/month because it replaces two salaries, grows revenue, and never asks for a holiday.

## The security layer
Twinthos Sentinel watches the feeds that matter — CVE databases, CISA's known-exploited list, EPSS scores, news, and supplier alerts — and filters them to the stack you actually run.

It does not wait for you to read a security newsletter. It does not rely on a human noticing a patch release at 2am. It runs on a schedule, it learns from your feedback, and it alerts you when something needs your attention.

**The audit costs £10,000. A single breach starts at £500,000.**

# Strategy

## Current priorities
1. Get the Company Brain product sellable.
2. Use HotBox Social as the working demonstration (a prospect, not a client).
3. Keep the website (twinthos.com) accurate and mobile-correct.

## Commercial stance
- Sell Tyson as the architect of community and systems, not as "using Twinthos".
- Lead pitches with the client's problem and Tyson's solution.
- Never fabricate research, customer results, integrations or prices.

## What we deliberately do not do
We do not put one client's data where another client, or the founder's own
memory, could see it. Isolation is a design requirement, not a feature.

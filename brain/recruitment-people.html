Bottlenecks · theirs07

Where the work actually jams.

  1. Timesheets. Photos on WhatsApp, retyped into Bullhorn, checked against shifts, exported to Xero.
  2. Right-to-work packs. 200–500 placements a month, each needing a compliant record.
  3. CV formatting. Same CV, rewritten into every client's template.
  4. Reference chasing. Email, wait, chase, wait.
  5. Bullhorn data entry. Every task keyed twice.

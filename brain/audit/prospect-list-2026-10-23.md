Together they cover £699m of accountancy fee income and £903m of recruitment and freight turnover, so every one of them already employs the admin the offer replaces.

## Method, and what is checked

- **Candidates** come from two published league tables, not from memory: the Accountancy Age Top 50+50 (2025 fee income and partner counts) and the Companies House league tables on koraso.com (turnover from the latest filed accounts, refreshed 23 October 2026).
- **The named person** is taken from the firm's own site or from a dated press article about their appointment, then re-fetched and checked for the surname. 23 of 24 confirmed by a scripted re-fetch; the 24th (Menzies) sits behind a page that blocks scripted requests and was confirmed from a rendered extract of the same article.
- **A dated trigger** is only recorded when a page states it. Where none exists the row says so; the job is still real, there is simply nothing fresh to hang the opener on.
- **The job** is the repeated piece of work every client or placement generates. It is an inference from the sector's business model, not a client quote. Anyone who tells you otherwise in a first call is right and the row gets rewritten.

## Score, and the arithmetic

Sector base (from `probability-ranking.json`: accountancy 8.1, recruitment 8.0, freight 7.7) + fee growth (+0.6 above 50%, +0.4 for 25–50%, +0.2 for 10–25%, −0.2 if shrinking) + 0.7 if a dated trigger exists, −2.0 where the decision has moved to a parent. Capped at 10, floor 3.

## What I did not verify

- **The pain is inferred, not quoted.** No client of these firms has publicly described the queue. The job is what the sector's billing model forces; the first call is the test.
- **Office lists** are only printed where a contact page exposes postcodes. Five rows say “not confirmed from a scriptable page” because the firm renders addresses in an image or a map. Do not read that as a single office.
- **Simon Massey's tenure** at Menzies dates from a November 2021 appointment notice. Confirm he is still the managing partner before you use his name in the opening line.
- **Nuvo names two leaders.** The same press coverage names Ben Knapp as managing director and Steve Sharp as founder and chief executive. Ask which one owns process before pitching.
- **Kreston Reeves is not a sale.** It joined AAB in 2026, so the budget moved. It is on the list to point you at AAB's integration work, scored 6.8 for that reason.

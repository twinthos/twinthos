## What this list is not

- Not a mailing list. No addresses, no phone numbers, no guessing at formats.
- Not a lead score. `warmth` in `target-register.json` is judgement, not observed buying intent.
- Not verified buying intent. Where a firm already ships its own agent on the same job, that is recorded in its entry.
- Not complete. Firms without a verifiable legal entity, a published headcount, or an anchor vacancy were excluded rather than described from second-hand snippets.

## The ghost-job pattern to avoid

The adverts that dominate a "trainee AI" search in Bath are not jobs:

- **IT Career Switch** — "Trainee AI Engineer", "Data Science Trainee", "Digital Marketing Trainee"
- **ITOL Recruit** — same titles, same text
- **Newto Training** — "AI Data Engineer"

Each one is classified by Reed itself as a "Training Course". They take an enquiry, then sell
a course or a placement scheme. They were 55 of the 245 cards in this sweep. Filter to
"Permanent"/"Contract" on any board and they disappear.

## Student finance at 40 — verified on gov.uk, 23 Oct 2026

**There is no upper age limit for Tuition Fee Loans or grants.** gov.uk words it exactly:
"There's no upper age limit for Tuition Fee Loans or grants." The age rule bites at 60, not
40: from 60 the maintenance loan drops to a capped amount (£4,582 for 2026/27).

Your year, by start date — the split matters, because England changes system on 1 January 2027:

| | Course starts before 1 Jan 2027 | Course starts on/after 1 Jan 2027 (LLE) |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition | up to £9,790 | £9,790 per 120 credits, £39,160 lifetime |
| Maintenance (away, outside London) | up to £10,830 | up to £15,415 per course year |
| One year, maximum | **£20,620** | **£25,205** |

- Both maintenance figures are household-income tested — that is a ceiling, not an offer.
- LLE maintenance needs at least £1,587.50 of tuition entitlement left and in-person attendance.
- Repayment: LLE courses repay above £25,000, written off 40 years after the first April due.
  Interest currently 4.1%.
- **The blocker is not your age, it is previous study.** gov.uk: "You'll usually only get
  student finance if you're doing your first higher education qualification — even if your
  previous course was self-funded." If you already hold a degree, funding is limited to
  named exceptions (topping up, part-time, specific healthcare/teaching routes).
- No-experience route without A-levels: Access to Higher Education Diploma, funded by an
  Advanced Learner Loan, which is written off once you complete an eligible HE course.

Sources: https://www.gov.uk/student-finance/who-qualifies ·

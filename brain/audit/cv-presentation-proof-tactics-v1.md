# Founder CV: presentation and proof tactics a monochrome two-page A4 PDF can carry

Checked against live pages on 30 Oct 2026. Each tactic lists a source, its date, a short quote, the risk to ATS text extraction, and how to apply it to the approved monochrome style: Inter font, black or white ground, 17pt body, two A4 pages, name stacked at 104pt.

These are tactics typical applicants do not use well. All 11 fit on two A4 pages and stay readable by a parser when built as plain selectable text.

---

## 1. Short "Now" line (present-focus headline)

- **Source:** https://www.cvmaker.com/blog/resume-writing/resume-headline
- **Date:** 23 December 2025
- **Quote (20 words):** "...focus on the past and present... what you've achieved and what you can do at this present point in time."
- **ATS risk:** Low. It is plain left-aligned text under the name and adds keywords. Risk only if set inside a text box or as a graphic, which a parser cannot read.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** One line in Inter at 17pt (or slightly larger), black on white, directly under the stacked 104pt name. Keep it to a single line so the two-page budget holds. No box, no fill, so it stays selectable.

## 2. "What I am looking for" targeting line (career objective)

- **Source:** https://www.jobalign.app/en/blog/what-is-a-career-objective-statement
- **Date:** 17 August 2026
- **Quote (19 words):** "...names the role you want, the skill or credential you bring, and the value you'll deliver."
- **ATS risk:** Low if it mirrors keywords from the posting in plain text. Risk if it names a role that fights the job title the parser files you under, or pushes real content off the two pages.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** One Inter 17pt line under the Now line, black on white. Fit it to one target from the skills audit (for example, Head of AI adoption). One line only, so summary and experience keep their space.

## 3. Live web version of the CV with the same text

- **Source:** https://path.cv/blog/resume-to-website-guide
- **Date:** 2026 (no date on page; retrieved 30 Oct 2026)
- **Quote (23 words):** "A resume website is always current, lives at one link you can put anywhere, and is readable by both people and search engines."
- **ATS risk:** None to the PDF. The site is not the file you upload; an ATS reads the uploaded PDF, not the web page. Keep the page text identical so the match score is the same.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** Point the header at twinthos.com, already live. The two-page PDF stays the submission file. The web twin mirrors the same text so a human can go deeper without the PDF drifting out of date.

## 4. QR code paired with the printed URL

- **Source:** https://www.qrstuff.com/feeds/blog/qr-code-resume
- **Date:** 25 June 2026
- **Quote (10 words):** "ATS software treats QR codes as images and ignores them."
- **ATS risk:** High if the QR is the only link, because a parser reads it as an image and extracts nothing. Always pair it with the full URL written out as plain selectable text.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** Print the QR at about 2cm square in pure black on white, next to twinthos.com, with the full URL in Inter 17pt plain text beside it. Test it on a printed copy before sending. One code, in the header.

## 5. Links to public work as proof

- **Source:** https://www.jobalign.app/en/blog/links-in-ats-resume
- **Date:** 2 September 2026
- **Quote (19 words):** "...the ones that back up a claim you already made in your work history or show proof of skill."
- **ATS risk:** Low if each link is a labeled full URL in plain text (twinthos.com, github.com/twinthos). Risk if links are clickable only with no visible URL, or a parser strips the metadata and the string is lost.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** In the header, list twinthos.com and github.com/twinthos as labeled, full, plain-text URLs in Inter 17pt, black on white. Keep to two to four links. Make them clickable in the PDF too, but never rely on the click alone.

## 6. Problem-action-result (PAR) bullets

- **Source:** https://careereducation.wellesley.edu/resources/how-write-effective-resume-bullets
- **Date:** No date shown (career-office resource; retrieved 30 Oct 2026)
- **Quote (19 words):** "Problem - Action - Result... what problem were you seeking to address? What action did you take? What were the results?"
- **ATS risk:** Low. PAR is wording, not layout. Keep one bullet per line with a plain bullet character. No embedded tables or columns that could scramble clause order.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** Rewrite each Twinthos and online-school line as Problem, Action, Result in Inter 17pt, black on white, one bullet each. Shape: the gap or defect (problem), what was built (action), the measurable close (result). Keep every claim to one that the evidence file grades A or B.

## 7. DOCX twin with identical text for ATS

- **Source:** https://www.atsgrade.com/blog/resume-file-format-pdf-vs-docx-ats-2026
- **Date:** 2026 (title year; no explicit date on page)
- **Quote (18 words):** "Keep the content identical so your match score does not depend on which file you happened to send."
- **ATS risk:** The DOCX must carry the same selectable text as the PDF. Risk if it uses text boxes, tables, or header/footer content that older parsers drop. One master text, exported both ways, removes the drift.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** Keep one Inter-font source. Export the two-page A4 PDF for humans and a single-column .docx with identical wording for portals that ask for Word. Layout can relax for parsing, but the text must match line for line.

## 8. Single number band

- **Source:** https://goodspace.ai/resume-examples/inventory-manager
- **Date:** 2026 (no date on page)
- **Quote (19 words):** "Key achievements | Optional band under the summary | 3 to 4 headline metrics... pulled out where they are seen first."
- **ATS risk:** Medium. A band built as a table, text box, or image flattens to nothing in a parser. Build it as plain left-aligned text lines, not a graphic, so the number stays selectable.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** One band under the summary carrying a single hard number, for example "around 20 million organic views a month" (about-page figure, grade A). Render it as plain Inter 17pt text on the white ground, not a shaded box, so it parses and still anchors the eye.

## 9. Plain-text self-test (paste into Notepad)

- **Source:** https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/resume-ai
- **Date:** 2 October 2025
- **Quote (20 words):** "Copy your entire resume into Notepad... If the text appears out of order or sections are missing, try simplifying your formatting."
- **ATS risk:** This is a check, not a change. It reveals dropped text, scrambled order, or an image-only page before the employer's parser does. Zero added risk; it lowers risk.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** Before each send, copy the exported PDF into a plain-text editor and confirm the 104pt name, twinthos.com, the github handle, dates, and each role title survive in order. Fix anything that breaks, then re-export.

## 10. Standard section headings and single-column reading order

- **Source:** https://rezoom.io/ats-resume-format/
- **Date:** 2026 (October; no explicit article date on page)
- **Quote (18 words):** "standard headings, full job titles, one date style and plain bullets, saved as .docx or a text-based PDF."
- **ATS risk:** This is the main defense. Creative headings ("Where I've Made an Impact") and multi-column layouts make parsers miss or scramble fields. Plain headings and one column keep reading order intact.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** Use the headings Summary, Experience, Skills, Education in that order, one column top to bottom. Keep the 104pt stacked name and the 17pt body on a single text flow so the parser reads the name, then each section, in order. The big name must be real text, not an image.

## 11. Contact details in the document body, not the header/footer

- **Source:** https://atsverification.com/blog/ats-friendly-resume-format-guide-2026/
- **Date:** 8 May 2026 (updated 17 Oct 2026)
- **Quote (9 words):** "No: tables, columns, headers/footers, text in images, fancy graphics."
- **ATS risk:** High. Many parsers skip the page header and footer regions, so a contact block placed there (common in designed CVs) is dropped and the holder becomes unreachable. Put contact in the body.
- **Apply to monochrome style:** Place twinthos@agentmail.to, twinthos.com, github.com/twinthos, and the Bath / Bristol location in the body under the name, not in a PDF header or footer. Keep them in the same Inter 17pt flow so they parse on both pages.

---

## How the 11 fit on two A4 pages

- Page 1: stacked 104pt name, contact-in-body (11), Now line (1), targeting line (2), web twin plus labeled links (3, 5), single number band (8), summary.
- Page 2: experience in PAR bullets (6), skills, education. QR code (4) and DOCX twin (7) sit with the header set and the export step. Plain-text self-test (9) and single-column standard headings (10) are build rules that apply across both pages.

Every tactic stays monochrome, uses Inter, keeps 17pt body text, and holds to two A4 pages. Every one reads cleanly when the name, links, and band are selectable text rather than images.

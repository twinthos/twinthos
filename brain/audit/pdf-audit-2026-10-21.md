## The eight defects that matter

1. **Sources were not clickable.** Five documents, dozens of footnoted sources,
   `0 /URI` annotations. On a phone that means retyping a domain to check a claim.
   Truth before persuasion fails at the first verification attempt.
2. **Letter-spacing was baked into the text layer.** `pdftotext` returns
   `T W I N T H O S · TA R G E T L I S T` and, in audit-report, a citation URL
   rendered as `D , A R T I C L E I D = 1 3 9`. Search, copy-paste and screen
   readers break; the source can no longer be copied out of the document.
3. **Footers failed contrast.** Page furniture was `#A9A196` on `#FCFAF6` =
   **2.45:1**, and `#4E635F` on `#0D1A18` = **2.78:1**. WCAG AA needs 4.5:1.
   These are the page numbers and running labels a reader uses to navigate.
4. **Page economy was poor.** who-to-target-v2 spends 24 A4 pages on roughly
   1,300 words — about 55 words per page. Text stopped 90–125mm short of the
   footer on most pages: a third to a half of every page was empty.
5. **No build script.** Each PDF was hand-written HTML printed by hand. Nothing
   recorded the command, the source, or the version; no timestamp in the filename.
6. **Structural risk.** `.page{height:297mm;overflow:hidden}` with absolutely
   positioned footers: a block that grows 5mm loses its last line silently. There
   is no error path in print-to-PDF.
7. **Metadata was a Title and nothing else.** No Author, no Subject, no Keywords,
   and a `Creator` that names the browser. File > Properties in a client's viewer
   showed the pipeline, not the firm.
8. **Type sizes were tuned by hand.** 14 to 24 distinct sizes per document, with
   no scale. Footnotes at 10pt render at 6.3pt effective on a 375pt-wide phone.

## The gate, as a standing rule

No PDF goes to Telegram until `pdf_gate.py` exits 0. It fails a document that has:
glyphs outside the safety margin, glyphs within 6mm of the page edge (the
clipping signature), any non-final page under 55% full, text under 9pt, more than
9 type sizes, letter-spaced extraction, zero links, missing Author, a browser UA
in Creator, or any text colour below 4.5:1 against the background it actually
sits on.

## What the five-reviewer swarm added, and what it got wrong

The swarm read the same five files independently. Six findings were checked by
hand and hold:

1. **A fabricated client result was sitting in a sendable cold email.**
   `who-to-target.pdf` p9: “One Bristol bureau cut a two-day run to about four
   hours,” with a note on the same page saying to delete it if the case study had
   not happened. It had not (`services.md`: no client has been delivered these).
   Removed from the send path.
2. **A withdrawn recommendation was still in a PDF a client could forward.**
   `what-twinthos-is.pdf` prices block management at £2,500/mo and £5,000/mo —
   the sector the audit withdraws. The PDF predates the correction to its own
   `.md` (`CORRECTED 21 OCT 2026 — DO NOT ACT ON THIS DOCUMENT`) by 4,958 seconds.
   The gate now fails a PDF older than its source, and this file fails it.
3. **`recruitment-targets.pdf` does not contain the string “Twinthos” in any
   extraction mode.** The company's own name was unsearchable in its own document.
4. **Two page-number systems per page, one apart.** A circled numeral top-right
   and a footer numeral bottom-right, drifting (page 9 shows 09/09).
5. **Georgia and Helvetica Neue were never installed.** Every file embeds DejaVu
   Serif and Liberation Sans; `fc-match Georgia` resolves to DejaVuSerif. The
   files are portable because fonts are embedded, but the pipeline was not.
6. **Chrome never exited 0.** Each build ran 120–124s against a timeout kill and
   success was inferred from a file appearing. Reproduced: exit 124 with a fresh
   profile, exit 21 with a locked one.

One swarm claim did **not** hold and is recorded here as rejected:
`what-twinthos-is.pdf` does **not** contain “Principal Accountable Person” or
“legal defensibility” — `pdftotext | grep -i` returns nothing for either. The
stale-sector and stale-price findings are real; the specific legal-claim finding
was not reproducible.

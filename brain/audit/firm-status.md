## The one that closed

**Taylor Rose MW** is the largest HMLR filer in the register and was the cleanest case for a desk that drives existing screens. It is a closing window: the firm's own news and a consultant page record a migration to Salesforce through summer 2026. An estate with integrations weakens the reason to buy a screen-driving desk every month. Call it, but sell the £999 audit rather than a deployment.

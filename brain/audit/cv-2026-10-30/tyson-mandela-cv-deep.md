## What I do

**Specify and run bespoke AI agents.** I write the operating rules first — inputs, decision rights, escalation, and what the agent must never do — then build it inside the tools a team already owns. The model is a commodity input; the brain, the job definition, and the history are not.

**Automate the work underneath.** Python, Flask, FastAPI, REST, OAuth, webhooks, JSON/CSV pipelines, key-free scrapers, cron, Git, Linux, static-site and CDN operation. TypeScript/Next.js for the web layer.

**Prove the output before it ships.** Release gates, machine checks, source re-fetching, contrast and overflow QA. If it cannot be checked, it does not ship.

**Audit a live system and name what is real.** Separate true defects from platform limits. Drive local findings to zero and report the rest plainly.

**Ship documents and sites at volume.** A 526-line measured-PDF pipeline holds a house style across hundreds of pages. The live site runs 289 commits in 18 days, gated and verified.

**Research a market to a named person.** Every company, every decision-maker, every claim traced to the page it came from and re-checked before publication.

**Teach AI in plain English.** I explain hard technical ideas to non-technical audiences, removing the fear without removing the rigour.

## How I develop my AI practice

**Model testing.** I follow model releases, agent tools, and security developments, then test their relevance to real work. I compare capability, output quality, and cost across Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, and open-weight models — not to chase the newest name, but to pick the right tool for the job spec.

**Structured prompting and tool orchestration.** Practical understanding of context, tool use, memory, and model limitations. I use structured instructions, iterative testing, and critical review to improve every system I build.

**Learning through breaking.** I enjoy finding out why something fails and working through it until the result improves. The CV you are reading was stress-tested against a 16-check machine gate; the site auditor runs 24 checks per page.

**Security and oversight.** I apply AI security auditing to agent workflows — reviewing data access, automated actions, and operating boundaries. A named human signs off consequential decisions. I have found and closed real exposure on my own infrastructure.

## Methods

**Loop engineering** — write the pass test first, fix, re-run the identical script, and treat the difference between runs as the proof.

**The one page** — one job per page, one price ladder, a clickable link for every doubtable claim, average sentence under 15 words.

**Five-factor scoring** — weighted criteria with caps, floors and written reasons for every exclusion.

**Source-before-claim** — every named person, company, and number is re-fetched before it ships. If no document supports a fact, it is marked unknown.

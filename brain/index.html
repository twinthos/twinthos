Twinthos Brain

Your company, understood.

Twinthos Brain turns the knowledge scattered across your emails, documents, projects and people into one organised company intelligence, then connects it to work your digital employee carries out under your control.

  • Persistent memoryApproved decisions, agreed preferences and project history retained across the team and the system.

How it works

Ingest. Organise. Query. Act.

A managed engagement, not a product you wire up yourself. The order below is the order it happens.

01

Ingest the approved knowledge

We work with you to identify the sources: documents, inboxes, project records, CRM, meeting notes. We connect only what you approve and bring it into one controlled store.

02

Organise by your business, not ours

Knowledge is grouped by your projects, your procedures and your people. Permissions, retention and ownership are set by you and recorded in the system.

03

Authorised people can ask

Your team and your digital employee can both ask the brain, with sources. The brain does not invent memories, share across clients or change approved policy without a logged approval.

04

Connected to work, under your control

Where you have a managed digital employee, the brain prepares, drafts and updates approved workflows. Anything outside the agreed scope is returned to your team for decision.

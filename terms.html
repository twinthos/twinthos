Terms

The terms that govern
the Twinthos service.

These terms apply to the Twinthos managed service. Your proposal may set out additional or more specific terms for your workflow.

Service definition

What Twinthos provides.

1. The managed service

Twinthos provides a managed AI operations service. We design, implement, monitor and maintain AI agents that carry out agreed operational work across your existing systems. The service is provided under the terms below.

2. The proposal

Before activation, we provide a written proposal setting out the work covered, the systems involved, the approval points, the success measures, the support arrangements and any separately charged software or additional work. The proposal forms part of the service and may set out terms additional to these.

3. Fees and billing

The managed service is £5,000 per month, exclusive of VAT where applicable. Billing begins when your workflow is deployed and running. The minimum engagement is 3 months. After the minimum term, either side may end the service with 30 days' written notice. Fees are payable in advance each month.

4. Separately charged work

Any work beyond the agreed scope — additional workflows, system integrations, reporting outside the agreed measures, or software costs — will be set out in writing and agreed before it is done.

5. What you provide

You provide access to the systems the workflow uses, confirm the rules in writing, make the decisions reserved for your team and keep those rules current. You are responsible for the accuracy of the information your systems provide and the decisions your team makes.

6. Support

Support covers the agreed scope of the workflow and its operation. It is provided by the named operator during working hours and does not include 24-hour coverage or emergency response. Service issues are handled as they arise within the agreed scope.

7. Limitations

Twinthos does not guarantee specific sales, savings or other financial results. The service performs the work described in your proposal. Outcomes depend on factors including demand, pricing, delivery and your team's handling of reserved decisions.

8. Data and service end

When the service ends, we wind down the workflow — revoke credentials, document what was done and handle the data per the service-end process agreed for your setup. You can ask us to delete data at any time, subject to legal or contractual retention requirements.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Material changes will be notified in writing. Your proposal may set out additional terms specific to your workflow.

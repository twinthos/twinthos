How it works

From manual coordination to managed operation.

Four stages. One operational layer. We map your work, set boundaries, assume the repeatable parts, and run the workflow across your existing systems.

The four stages

Observe, define, hand over, operate.

Stage 01

Observe

We map how the work actually happens — people, systems, information, actions, triggers, rules, approvals, exceptions, human boundaries.

Stage 02

Define

We establish rules, permissions and human boundaries. What Twinthos can do alone. What requires a person. Where the escalation lives.

Stage 03

Hand over

Twinthos assumes the repeatable parts. The workflow runs across your existing systems. No new dashboard. No new platform.

Stage 04

Operate

The workflow runs, exceptions are handled, performance is monitored. Continuous improvement. Human review at the boundary. Nothing disappears without being measured first.

What stays human

Control authority stays with you. The agent operates within the rules you set — approved workflows, approved systems, approved communications. Anything outside those rules pauses and comes to you. Judgement, approval, exception and strategic decisions stay human.

One process, owned end to end

An enquiry, followed through.

Enquiry received

A new enquiry arrives by email: "Can we view the Albert Road unit this week?"

Agent action

Checks availability, finds Thursday 6pm and Saturday 10am open. Books the Thursday slot. Sends confirmation. Updates the customer record. Notifies the team.

Complete

Viewing confirmed. Customer record updated. Team notified. No follow-up needed.

Approval — the point

A 15% early-payment discount on invoice INV-2026-042 is requested — below the 20% approval rule. The agent pauses. No discount applied. Customer notified. Awaiting your decision.

Human involvement: exception only.

Who's responsible

The person designing your operation stays responsible for it.

Twinthos deployments are currently founder-led, from workflow design through live operation. No anonymous handover. No unnecessary layers between the client and the person responsible for the workflow.

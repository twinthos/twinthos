How it works

From work someone has to remember to work that moves.

We take responsibility for one clearly defined job at a time, built into the tools you already use, with the decisions that need a person kept with your team.

01The mechanism

Understand · Define · Connect · Test · Manage.

Five steps, in plain English. Understand the process, define the job, connect the tools, test the actions, manage the work. The first four prepare the agent. The fifth is what you keep paying for.

02The authority line

Below the line, the agent acts.
Above it, your team decides.

The line is written down before activation: which actions the agent may take alone, which need approval, and who receives each exception.

Authority line

Within agreed rules

  • Approved messages and follow-ups
  • Recording information in agreed systems
  • Requesting missing details
  • Assigning and scheduling the next action

Always a person

  • Pricing, discounts and refunds beyond limits
  • Complaints and disputes
  • Anything outside the written rules
  • Changes to scope, access or rules
03Responsibilities

Who does what, in writing.

Your team

Provides access and makes reserved decisions

Grants approved access, confirms the rules, keeps them current and handles the decisions above the line.

Twinthos

Builds, tests and manages the workflow

Designs and configures the workflow, tests it, monitors it, maintains connections and handles issues within the agreed scope.

Both

Judge whether it is worth the fee

Success measures are agreed before activation and reviewed against what the workflow actually handled.

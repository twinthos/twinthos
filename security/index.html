Questions we answer in writing before activation

Five things, documented before anything runs.

Not a policy page. A list of the questions we answer in writing for every client before a workflow is activated. The answers are specific to your setup — not generic.

1. Access

What information is accessed, why it is needed, and which actions require approval.

We document the specific data each workflow needs, why it needs it, and which actions are within the agent's authority versus which require a person's approval. Nothing is accessed that the workflow doesn't need.

2. Processing

Where data is hosted, which providers and models are involved, and their actual training settings.

The specific providers, hosting locations and model configurations your workflow uses are documented per client. We do not make claims about aggregate data handling — we document what your specific setup uses.

3. Separation

How clients are separated, and how credentials are granted and revoked.

How client environments are isolated, how credentials are issued and scoped, and how access is revoked when a workflow ends or a team member changes.

4. Records

What activity records exist, how retention, export and deletion work.

What the service logs, how long it keeps those records, how you can export them, and how deletion is handled when a workflow ends or you request it.

5. Incidents

Who handles incidents and what happens when the service ends.

How incidents are escalated, who is responsible, and the process for winding down a workflow — including what happens to the data, the credentials, and the records.

A note on claims

We do not claim UK-only processing. A UK machine location does not establish how every provider involved in a workflow processes data. If that distinction matters for your business, tell us and we'll document exactly what each provider in your specific setup does.

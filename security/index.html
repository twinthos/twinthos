Security

You should know
what Twinthos can do.

Before any workflow is activated, you should understand the information it needs, the actions it can take and the people responsible for the service.

What can it see?

The information the workflow needs to do its job — the files, messages and records it must read to complete its tasks. We document exactly what is accessed and why, before activation.

What can it change?

The actions the workflow is permitted to take: sending messages, updating records, making appointments, moving information. Every action is within the agreed rules for that workflow.

What needs approval?

The actions that require a person before they happen — spending above a limit, exceptions to the standard process, anything outside the agreed rules. These are identified before deployment and documented in the audit.

What happens if something goes wrong?

The workflow pauses when it cannot continue safely within its instructions. Issues are reported to the person responsible for the service, who handles them directly. The managed service includes issue handling and a review process.

How is access removed?

Credentials are granted per workflow and can be revoked at any time. When the service ends, access is removed and the working data is exportable on request.

What is recorded?

Activity records show what the workflow did — the actions taken, the messages sent and the decisions made. Retention, export and deletion are documented per client before activation.

The specifics — providers, locations, retention periods — are documented per client, because they depend on the systems your workflow uses. We do not claim UK-only processing: a UK machine location does not establish how every provider processes data.

Your privacy notice and commercial terms are written to match the implemented service, not a generic template.

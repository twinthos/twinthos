Before an agent operates in your business, you should understand the information it needs, the actions it can take and the people responsible for the service.

Questions we answer in writing before activation

Access

What information is accessed, why it is needed, and which actions require approval.

Processing

Where data is hosted, which providers and models are involved, and their actual training settings.

Separation

How clients are separated, and how credentials are granted and revoked.

Records

Incidents

Who handles incidents and what happens when the service ends.

Security and data

Know what has access. Know who is responsible.

Before an agent operates in your business, you should understand the information it needs, the actions it can take and the people responsible for the service. The specifics depend on your systems, so we document them for each client.

01Answered in writing

Five questions, answered before anything runs.

Not a policy page. The answers are specific to your setup, and you see them before a workflow is activated.

01 · Access

What is accessed, why, and which actions need approval

The data each workflow needs and the reason for it, and which actions sit within the agent’s authority versus which require a person.

02 · Processing

Where data is hosted and which providers and models are involved

The providers, hosting locations and model settings your workflow actually uses, including their training settings.

03 · Separation

How clients are separated and credentials are granted and revoked

How environments are isolated, how credentials are scoped, and how access ends when a workflow or a team member changes.

04 · Records

What activity is recorded, and how retention, export and deletion work

What the service logs, for how long, how you can export it and how deletion is handled.

05 · Incidents

Who handles incidents and what happens when the service ends

Escalation, responsibility and the wind-down: data, credentials and records.

A note on claims

We do not claim UK-only processing. A UK machine location does not establish how every provider in a workflow processes data. If that matters for your business, we will document exactly what each provider in your setup does.

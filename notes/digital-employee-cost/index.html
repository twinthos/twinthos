How much does a digital employee cost vs a part-time VA in 2026?

The headline comparison is £5,000 a month versus £2,400 a month. The real comparison is what each can actually do, the hours they recover, and who is responsible when something goes wrong.

The short answer: a part-time VA at £12 an hour for 20 hours a week costs roughly £1,040 a month and covers the tasks the owner assigns. A managed digital employee at £5,000 a month runs 24/7 inside your existing tools, under written rules, with one named operator accountable. They are not the same product.

When the VA wins

For a 3-person team with one owner doing most of the admin, a part-time VA at £1,040 a month is excellent value. The work is bounded, the VA can be trained in an afternoon, and the owner is the only person accountable either way.

When the digital employee wins

For a 10-to-30-person business where admin is recurring, lives inside multiple tools, and is losing the owner 11+ hours a week (per the Amex SME Barometer, July 2026), the calculus changes. The bottleneck is not "do we have hands." It is "do we have hands that work 24/7 inside Xero, HubSpot, and Gmail, under written rules, with one named person responsible."

The break-even for a 12-person business

If a managed digital employee at £5,000 a month recovers 11 hours of owner time a week and the owner bills at £120 an hour, the recovered capacity is £5,280 a month. The deal pays for itself from month one. The remaining question is whether the recovered hours go into margin, sales, or rest. The audit measures this per business.

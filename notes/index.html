Business

Why AI changes the economics of running a company.

Business Half of small businesses already let AI do a person’s job. Their teams are exhausted. Bluevine asked 729 owners: 53% say AI absorbed a full-time role, 75% plan to absorb more instead of hiring — and 65% say running lean wrecked their wellbeing. The deployment, not the automation, is what breaks people. 9 min read Business 95% of AI pilots fail. The number has been stable for two years. MIT, McKinsey and BCG independently found the same wall. The failure is almost never the model — it is the moment the output hits a handoff that still depends on a person remembering to push it. 12 min read

Models

Benchmarks, leaders, and what the leaderboards actually measure.

Models The AI that answers your phone now costs $2 a month. Almost nobody has switched. Google shipped its long-running agent at $2 per million input tokens on 14 October; OpenAI started rolling out always-on agents a week earlier. The economics of digital labour collapsed. The reasons businesses haven’t moved have nothing to do with price. 10 min read

UK owners say admin takes 11 hours a week. What is your week costing you?

A 2026 survey of 1,000 UK small business owners puts admin and finance at about 11 hours a week, almost twice the time they report on sales and growth.

UK small business owners report about 11 hours a week on administrative and finance tasks, roughly six working days a month. They report about 3.6 days a month on sales and business development. More than half (54 percent) say paperwork gets in the way of running the business.

In this guide

    The figure

    The American Express SME Business Barometer, fielded by Opinium, surveyed 1,000 owners of UK micro, small and medium-sized businesses. Respondents estimated an average of 11 hours a week on administrative or finance tasks, about six working days a month [1]. They reported about 3.6 days a month on sales and business development [1][2].

    More than half of owners (54 percent) say paperwork gets in the way of running their business [1].

    What it costs, on an illustrative rate

    Survey estimates are not the same as accounts. As an illustration only, if an owner's time is worth £120 an hour, 11 hours a week is about £1,320 a week, or £68,640 a year. That rate is our assumption, not a survey figure. Put your own hourly value in and the sum changes in proportion.

    The more useful question is which of those hours are repeatable. Chasing invoices, re-entering data between two systems, sending reminders and booking the same follow-up are the usual candidates.

    What to do with the number

    OUR READING: the first step is measurement. Log every admin task for one week, note how long it takes and how often it repeats. The tasks that repeat most and need the least judgement are the ones worth handing to software or a person with a clear brief.

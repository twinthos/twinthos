How many hours a week do UK SMB owners spend on admin in 2026?

Eleven. Nearly twice the time they spend growing the business. Where the number comes from, what counts as admin, and what it costs a 12-person firm in lost margin.

In this guide

The short answer: 11 hours a week per UK small or medium-sized business owner, on tasks that do not grow the business. The figure comes from the American Express SME Business Barometer, fielded in July 2026 across 1,000 UK SMB owners.

What counts as admin in the Barometer

The Barometer lists the recurring tasks SMBs hand back to themselves: chasing invoices, replying to the same email twice, copying data between Xero and HubSpot, updating a rota, sending a reminder, logging a call, filing receipts, chasing a signature, rebooking a slot, tidying an inbox. Each one is small. The Barometer's point is that together they fill the working week.

What the same hours cost a 12-person business

If an owner bills their time at £120 an hour (a conservative UK SMB rate for sales, account management, or strategic work) and the 11 hours are recovered, that is £1,320 a week in freed capacity. Over a year, £68,640. The owner does not need to bill all of them. They need to stop losing all of them.

For a 12-person business turning over £1.8m, recovering 11 owner-hours a week is roughly 4% of turnover, returned as either margin or reinvested sales time.

Why the number matters for AI agent decisions

The Barometer's 11 hours is the demand side of the digital employee pitch. It is also the bar. If a managed AI agent does not, in writing, recover at least the hours it costs, the deal fails. The Twinthos operations audit exists to write that down before anything is paid for.

What the Barometer does not say

It does not say which hours are recoverable by software and which need the owner. It does not separate admin from compliance, or admin from sales follow-up. The recoverable hours for any one business depend on its tools and its rules. That is what the audit measures.

Frequently asked

Is 11 hours a week an average or a median?

The Barometer reports an average across surveyed owners. The median is likely lower; the long tail of owners spending 20+ hours a week pulls the average up.

Does this include email?

Yes. The Barometer includes email triage and replies in its definition of admin.

Does it include meetings?

Internal meetings are excluded. Customer calls and external meetings are included where they are recurring and administrative (e.g. rebooking, follow-up).

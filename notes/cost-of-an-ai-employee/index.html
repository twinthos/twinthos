What an AI employee really costs per hour

Dividing a monthly fee by 24 hours looks clever. It tells you almost nothing about the cost of a job done correctly. Here's what to count instead.

The software might run through the night. That doesn't mean every hour creates value. Count finished work, review time and the failures that take someone away from their own job.

The short answer

Twinthos lists a managed digital employee at £5,000 per month. That fee alone does not tell you the cost per correct result. Add setup, the work your people still check, exceptions, repairs and support, then divide the total by successfully completed tasks—not hours of uptime.

The software is the cheap part

Google's Gemini 4 Argon went live on 13 October 2026 at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. A token is about three quarters of a word, so a million tokens is roughly 750,000 words. A long customer enquiry, with the customer's history attached, runs to a few thousand input tokens. Hundreds of those in a month land in the tens of dollars.

That is the number people use to argue AI is nearly free. True, and almost useless. A month of usage for a five person business costs about what a team lunch costs. It tells you nothing about whether the work gets done.

The fee is the part you sign

At £5,000 a month, the listed managed service costs £60,000 over twelve months. A digital employee can be scheduled to work around the clock, but that does not make every hour a productive hour. If several tasks run at once, you still need to measure each correct outcome and the time a person spent checking it. A simple hourly division cannot capture that.

The National Living Wage in October 2026 is £12.71 an hour. However you do the sum, that gap is what this article sits inside. And the fee is not padding. Connecting your email, diary, CRM and accounting, then teaching it what may be promised and who gets told, takes skills most small firms do not have on the payroll.

The eleven hours nobody chose

The American Express SME Business Barometer in July 2026 found UK small business owners and staff spend an estimated 11 hours a week on admin and finance. Eleven hours is a working day and a half, every week, forever.

In a small business that work is nobody's job and everybody's second job. The owner does it at ten at night. The office manager does it between everything else. Set the three person cost below against £60,000 a year and the lazy reading says the human is cheaper. It fails one check: those hours are duplicated across the team, and the software does not care whether it is Tuesday or three in the morning.

The cost that sinks the sum

Now the line most comparisons skip: checking. If a person has to approve every action before it counts, nothing has been removed. The work has been rearranged into a harder shape, because checking a stranger's work against your own memory of what is true is harder than doing it yourself.

So draw the line yourself. Anything that cannot hurt anyone if it fails can run without review. Anything that costs money or a customer gets a person. Keep most of the volume in the first group and the checking stays small.

What the hours become

Eleven hours a week back, per person, is about 570 hours a year. That is roughly fourteen working weeks. Across a team it is 143 hours a month that did not go into the product, the pitch, the hire or the sleep.

Nobody spends fourteen working weeks on a dashboard. They take the holiday they cancelled twice. They ship the second product that has been a note on a phone for three years. They hire the person they could not train because there were no hours left. They stop dreading Mondays.

Count your own hours before you believe any of this

The prices here are Twinthos list prices at October 2026: £5,000 a month with a three month minimum, and £999 for the Operations Audit. The wage figures are the official rate and the published survey, both linked below. None of it is a promise that you will save what this page suggests. Count your own hours before and after. That is the only number worth signing against.

What this means for you

The token bill is noise. The monthly fee is the visible cost and the review time is the hidden one. Before you sign, count the hours your team spends on admin in an ordinary month, then count how many of those a system could carry without a person checking each one. That second number is the only saving that is real.

Questions people actually ask

Is dividing the fee by 24/7 hours a useful comparison?

Not on its own. The service's availability is not the same as completed work. Include setup, supervision and rework, then compare the total against correctly finished tasks.

Where does the eleven hours a week come from?

The American Express SME Business Barometer, July 2026, which found UK small business owners and staff spend an estimated 11 hours a week on admin and finance. Secondary reporting agrees on the figure.

Is this a promise that we will save money?

No. The prices are Twinthos list prices at October 2026 and the wage figures are the official rate and the published survey. The only figure worth trusting is the one you count yourself.

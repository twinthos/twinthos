# Twinthos Email Templates

## Template 1: Plain Text First Touch (Cold Outreach)

**Subject:** quick question — removing repeat work from your team

**Body:**
```
hi,

been thinking about how teams like yours handle the repeat operational work that eats up hours every week — intake, routing, status updates, follow-ups, the stuff that happens over and over and doesn't need a human brain for it.

most software adds more work. another tool to configure. another dashboard to check. another process to maintain. we built Twinthos the other way: it takes the repeat operational work off your team's plate, so the humans can focus on the work that actually needs a human.

not another platform to migrate to. not another integration to maintain. just the work handled, time returned.

if that's worth a look, reply "yes" and i'll send over a 5-minute demo. no pitch deck. no 30-minute call. just show you what it does and you decide.

-[your name]
Twinthos
```

**Why it works:**
- Subject looks internal (lowercase, specific)
- First line is a true observation (proves not a blast)
- Under 100 words (skimmable in 15 seconds)
- One low-friction CTA ("reply yes")
- Sets expectations ("no pitch deck, no 30-min call")
- Plain text signature (name, company, phone — no logo, no icons)

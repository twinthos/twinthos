## 1. Content and distribution — expert

Daily on-camera video, short-form vertical. 20 million views a month at peak.
Underneath the number: hook construction, retention structure, pacing, thumbnail and
title framing, publishing cadence, platform-native editing decisions.

Commercial use: acquisition at zero media spend. This is the skill most AI founders lack.

## 2. Audience as an asset — expert

Built an Instagram account to 500,000 followers and sold it to Jason van Blerk
(The Human Garage). Separately ran a paid community at 2,000 students.

This is the rarest item on the page: not "grew an audience" but "built an audience,
monetised it, and exited it". Most operators never complete the third step.

## 3. Education product design — advanced

Designed every course, module and student journey in a 2,000-student community.

material for HumanitAI CIC.

## 4. Long-form authorship — advanced

Commercial use: authority content, thought leadership, ghost-free brand voice.

## 5. Brand, offer and funnel — advanced

## Authorship measured from git, not assumed

A swarm agent counted `git log --format='%an'` across the repositories. Result, and it matters
for every claim made on his behalf:

- `twinthos_live`, 289 commits: **humanarchitect 15**, Twinthos 91, OWL Agent 81, Hermes Agent 58,
  OWL 18, Twinthos Bot 14, Hermes 11, Twinthos Deploy 1.
- `img-host-audit`, 22 commits, all under `humanarchitect@protonmail.com` — his personal email.
- `twinthos_backup`, 1,317 commits, 100% Hermes Agent automation.
- The public `twinthos/twinthos` history is agent-identity; his own pushes stayed local.

So: he conceived the product, wrote the first landing page through roughly ten design iterations,
set positioning and pricing, and directed everything after. He did not hand-write the site code,
the doc pipeline, the scrapers, the auditor or the backup system.

That distinction is the difference between "I built a platform" (defensible) and "I coded a
platform" (fails one probing question). Hold the line in the CV: *Python-literate direction*.

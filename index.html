06Questions

Asked, answered.

Is Twinthos a chatbot?+
No. A chatbot answers one message and forgets you. Twinthos is a team of workers that owns a whole job: it answers, chases, books, files and follows up, remembers everything, and works around the clock.
What does it cost?+
£5,000 a month, flat. The whole team, setup, UK hosting, human supervision and a weekly report. No setup fees, no usage limits. A junior covers 37 hours a week; this team covers all 168. Per hour covered, it costs less than that junior once you add NI, pension and a desk.
What if it does not work for me?+
Fair worry: you are handing over work your business runs on. The first 7 days are a full trial: your workers go live, you watch every action, and if it is not for you, you pay £0. No setup fee, ever. After that, 30 days' notice cancels. We only keep clients who would choose us over the junior hire.
How long does it take to go live?+
14 days. Week one we learn your business. Week two we build your team and you watch them work with training wheels. From day 15, a human supervises them around the clock.
Will it sound like a robot?+
No. Twinthos learns your tone from your own files and replies to routine messages in your voice. You approve anything important before it goes out.
Is my data safe?+
Yes. Your data stays in the UK, is never used to train AI, and each client runs in an isolated environment you own. A human in the UK supervises everything it does.
Who is this for?+
UK businesses that lose money when nobody answers: estate agents, law firms, trades, recruiters, accountants, dentists. If a missed call or an unchased invoice costs you more than £5,000 a month, this pays for itself.

The commercial case

We look at how often the work happens, what it takes from your team and what changes if it is handled differently. That could mean reducing avoidable spending, creating capacity for additional work or improving follow-through on existing opportunities.

Different results from different work: Time recovered, cash saved and additional profit are different results. We agree what matters and how it will be assessed.

Essential buyer questions

Questions worth answering
before you decide.

Why would we pay monthly?

The service includes responsibility after implementation: monitoring the work, maintaining connections, handling issues and making agreed changes. Your proposal defines that ongoing scope.

Will this give my team more to manage?

Your team helps define the process, grants approved access and handles reserved decisions. Twinthos manages the agreed implementation and operation. We include the remaining review work when assessing the value.

We already have AI tools. What would Twinthos add?

We look at what still requires someone to carry information or actions between tools. If your existing setup already completes the process adequately, another service may add little value.

Can you guarantee more sales or savings?

Sales depend on demand, pricing and delivery as well as follow-up. We define the work Twinthos will perform and how its performance will be assessed. Any financial case should make its assumptions clear.

What happens when the agent needs help?

The agreed process identifies what requires review, who receives it and how the issue is handled. We test relevant exception cases before activation.

How is our information handled?

We document the required access, providers and data arrangements for your workflow before activation. Read the Security and Data page.

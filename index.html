06Questions

Asked, answered.

Is this just a chatbot?+
No. A chatbot answers one message and stops. These are six workers who each own a whole job: answering, chasing, booking, checking and reporting, every hour of every day.
What does it cost?+
£5,000 a month, flat. Everything included: the six workers, setup, UK hosting, human supervision and a weekly report. A junior covers 37 hours a week for about £15 an hour once you add NI, pension and a desk. This team covers all 168 hours for about £6.87 an hour.
What if it does not work for me?+
Your first 7 days live are a trial. You watch every action your workers take, and if they disappoint you, the bill is £0. No setup fee, ever. After the trial, 30 days notice ends it. We only keep clients who would choose us over the junior hire.
How fast can it start?+
14 days. Week one we learn your business: two hours of your time and your files. Week two we build your six workers and you watch them work with training wheels. From day 15, a human in the UK supervises them around the clock.
What if it makes a mistake in front of my customers?+
Everything important is held for your approval before it goes out, and a human in the UK supervises around the clock. In the first 7 days you watch every single action. If a worker ever gets something wrong in front of a customer, you hear about it from us first, not from the customer.
Will my customers know?+
No. Your workers learn your tone from your own files and write in your voice. You approve anything important before it goes out.
Where does my data live?+
In the UK. Your data never trains AI models, each client runs in an isolated environment you own, and a human in the UK supervises everything your workers do.
Who is behind this?+
Twinthos is built and operated by Tyson Architect, an automation engineer in Bath, England. You deal with one named person from first call to weekly report.
Is this a managed AI agent service?+
Yes. Twinthos is a fully managed AI agent service for UK SMEs. We build, host and supervise your AI workers in the UK; you own them and cancel with 30 days notice.

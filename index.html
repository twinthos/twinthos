FAQ

The questions buyers actually ask.

Is this actually replacing people?

No. Twinthos takes the repetitive operational work - enquiries, follow-ups, chasing, filing, checking - and gives it continuous coverage. Your people keep the judgement calls, the relationships and the decisions. Most clients use recovered capacity to grow rather than cut.

What happens if it makes a mistake?

Important actions can be routed through approval workflows first. Twinthos operates within the rules you configure, flags anything unusual and escalates rather than guessing. Every action is recorded so you can review what happened and tighten the rules.

Will it sound like us?

Yes - that is part of the setup. In the first two hours together we map your tone, your phrases and how you talk to customers. Workers write in your voice, not a generic assistant voice, and you review output during the trial week.

What systems can it connect to?

Email, calendars, documents, CRMs and the tools your business already runs on. If your team uses it in a browser, Twinthos can usually work with it. You don't replace your existing software - Twinthos works through it.

Can a human approve important actions?

Yes. You define what needs approval - payments, sensitive replies, anything with real stakes. Those actions wait for you; routine work flows through.

What happens to my data?

Your business runs in an isolated, UK-hosted environment. Your data, your rules, your business. Nothing is shared between clients, and you can ask what was recorded at any time.

How quickly can we go live?

14 days from kickoff. Day 1–2 is a two-hour mapping call and controlled first tasks; by Day 7 you are reviewing real workflow output in the live trial.

Can we stop after the trial?

Yes. If it doesn't work for your business in the first 7 days, you pay £0 and walk away. No lock-in beyond the month you have paid for.

Why does it cost £5,000?

Because you are buying continuous operational coverage - six specialised workers, 168 hours a week, managed and supervised by us - not a software seat. The real question is what the missed enquiries, late invoices and admin hours already cost you.

What kind of business gets the most value?

UK SMEs with steady enquiry flow and repetitive operational work - estate agencies, law firms, recruiters, accountants, trades and clinics. If missed enquiries or slow follow-up cost you money, this pays for itself.

Is this suitable for a 10-person company?

Often the smaller the team, the sharper the pain - there is no spare capacity to absorb admin. A 10-person company with constant enquiries is exactly where six always-on workers change the week.

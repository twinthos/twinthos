How it works

We start with the work, not the technology.

The starting point is always the same: find the task that keeps repeating, understand what it needs, and work out whether a managed agent would actually help. If it would, we build it. If it wouldn't, we say so.

First — understand the work

We map the recurring task: what arrives, what the outcome should be, which systems are involved, and where a person needs to stay in the loop. This is not a sales conversation. It's the work.

Then — agree the rules

In writing: what the agent can do autonomously, what it must check before acting, and what it must pause and ask about. No action outside the agreed scope. No surprises.

Then — deploy and manage

We build the workflow into your tools, monitor it, maintain it, and report on what it handled. You review the results and decide whether to continue. Not a pilot programme with a conversion target. A working service.

What management includes
Infrastructure

The systems the workflow runs on, monitoring, access control and credential management — configured and maintained as part of the service.

Maintenance

Changes to connected tools, rule updates, fixes when something breaks. We handle it; you don't maintain infrastructure.

Reporting

What the workflow handled, where it paused for a decision, and anything that needs your attention. Regular, readable, no filler.

Support

Direct contact with the person who designed and runs your workflows. Not a ticketing system. Not a team you have to route through.

Workflows

The work we take on.

These are the workflows we see most often. Not a menu to pick from — a sense of the shape of the work. Every workflow is scoped for the specific business that uses it.

Workflow 01

Enquiries to bookings

A new enquiry arrives — by email, form, or message. The agent checks availability, books the appointment, sends confirmation, updates the record, and notifies the team.

Uses: your calendar, your email, your CRM or record system.

Workflow 02

Follow-ups and next steps

Leads that went cold, enquiries that need a second touch, appointments that need a reminder. The agent tracks what needs a next action and takes it within the agreed rules.

Workflow 03

Invoice administration

Invoices that need checking, matching, chasing, or recording. The agent works within your approval rules — anything outside the threshold comes to a person.

Workflow 04

Client operations

Record updates, status changes, notifications, routine admin across your client-facing systems. Defined per client, scoped in writing before activation.

Where your team stays involved

The agent operates within the rules you set. Anything outside those rules — a discount below your approval threshold, a request to reach a new system, a decision that needs judgment — pauses and comes to you. No action is taken without the agreed authority.

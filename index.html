Workflows

The work we take on.

These are the workflows we see most often. Not a menu to pick from — a sense of the shape of the work. Every workflow is scoped for the specific business that uses it.

Workflow 01

Enquiries → confirmed next steps

A new enquiry arrives — by email, form, or message. The agent checks availability, coordinates the response, keeps the customer record current, and notifies the team where a person needs to stay involved.

Uses: your calendar, your email, your CRM or record system.

Workflow 02

Leads → timely follow-ups

Leads that went cold, enquiries that need a second touch, appointments that need a reminder. The agent tracks what needs a next action and takes it within the agreed rules — bringing replies needing judgement to your team.

Workflow 03

Invoices → organised administration

Invoices that need checking, matching, chasing, or recording. The agent works within your approval rules — anything outside the threshold comes to a person before action is taken.

Workflow 04

Client updates → consistent records

Record updates, status changes, notifications, routine admin across your client-facing systems. Defined per client, scoped in writing before activation.

Where your team stays in control

The agent operates within the rules you set. Anything outside those rules — a discount below your approval threshold, a request to reach a new system, a decision that needs judgement — pauses and comes to you. No action is taken without the agreed authority.

How it begins

Three steps from enquiry to managed workflow.

Step 01

Define the work

Choose a recurring workflow with a clear outcome. What arrives, what should happen, which systems are involved, where a person needs to stay in the loop. This is not a sales conversation — it's the work.

Step 02

Agree the boundaries

Document the tools, the permitted actions, and the decisions requiring your approval. The scope, the rules, and the boundary are written down before anything runs. No surprises.

Step 03

Build and manage

Connect the tools, test the outcome, and run the workflow. Then monitor, maintain, and report on what it handled. You review and decide whether to continue. Not a pilot programme with a conversion target. A working service.

What does Twinthos take on?

Repeat operational work with a clear outcome, usable information and agreed rules. Enquiries, follow-ups, scheduling and record updates are good places to start. We confirm the actual scope before deployment.

Will my team have to prompt every step?

Within the agreed workflow, the agent follows events and takes permitted next actions without a person prompting every step. Your team stays involved where judgement or approval is needed.

What does £5,000 per month include?

Implementation and ongoing management of the agreed workflows, with the infrastructure, monitoring, maintenance, reporting and support specified in your plan. Separately chargeable work or external software is agreed in advance.

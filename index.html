Assumptions

  • Hours per month = weekly hours × 4.33.
  • Recovered capacity = (weekly hours × handover proportion ÷ 100) − weekly review hours, then × 4.33.
  • Capacity value = recovered hours × hourly staff cost. It is a time equivalent, not automatic cash.
  • Additional gross profit = affected opportunities × conversion uplift × gross profit per customer. It is an estimate, not a measurement.
  • Monthly contribution after fees = cash savings + estimated gross profit − £5,000 fee − extra costs. Negative results are shown as-is.
  • Collected invoice amounts are never counted as new revenue.

Recovered time, cash savings and additional profit are shown separately. This estimate combines avoidable spending and potential additional gross profit. It is not a cash-flow forecast. An estimate is a starting point for a measured pilot.

Buyer questions.

What can Twinthos take on?

Start with recurring work that has a clear outcome, usable information and agreed rules. Enquiries, follow-ups, scheduling and record updates are good workflows to assess. We confirm the actual scope before deployment.

Will my team have to prompt every step?

Within the agreed workflow, the agent can follow events and take permitted next actions without a person prompting every step. Your team remains involved where the workflow requires judgement or approval.

What happens when something goes wrong?

The workflow should pause or escalate when it cannot continue safely within its instructions. The managed service includes issue handling and a review process. Your agreement specifies the contacts, support hours and response expectations.

Can it use our existing software?

We assess the systems your workflow depends on and confirm supported actions before the pilot. Compatibility depends on access, integration support and the reliability of the specific workflow.

Can we approve important actions?

Yes. Approval requirements are part of the agreed workflow. Identify the actions, spending limits and exceptions that must wait for a person before activation.

How quickly can we start?

We confirm a deployment date after agreeing the scope and receiving the required access and information. The seven-day pilot begins when the agreed workflow is activated, not when the first call takes place.

What does £5,000 include?

Implementation and management of the agreed workflows, with the infrastructure, monitoring, maintenance, reporting and support specified in your plan. Any separately chargeable work or external software is agreed in advance.

What happens after the pilot?

You review the results and choose whether to continue. The pilot ends without a managed-service charge unless you opt in. An optional paid operations audit remains a separate purchase.

How is business data handled?

Before activation, we document the information the workflow needs, the systems that process it and the access granted. Review the security and privacy information for the confirmed data locations, providers and retention arrangements.

Is the £999 audit required?

No. It is useful when several workflows need assessment or you need a wider implementation plan. If the first workflow and its business case are already clear, we can assess it through the fit-call route.

Who is this most suitable for?

Businesses with enough recurring operational work and commercial value to justify a managed service. The first discussion checks the opportunity, the systems involved and the people needed to make it work.

Will this replace employees?

Twinthos takes on agreed work. How you use the recovered capacity is a business decision. Assess the service against actual workload, service quality and economics rather than a promised number of roles replaced.

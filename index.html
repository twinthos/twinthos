01The problem

The work is small.
The interruption isn’t.

Checking whether someone replied. Finding the missing detail. Updating the record. Asking again. Each takes a minute. Together they pull people away from customers, delivery and the work that needs their expertise.

  • Monday, 09:02Did anyone reply to them?
  • Monday, 11:40Can you chase this?
  • Tuesday, 15:15Has the record been updated?
  • Wednesday, 10:30Who owns this now?
  • Friday, 16:55Can you remind me next week?

The name for it

That is operational drag.

The repeated chasing, checking and updating that keeps a business dependent on someone’s attention. When everyone is busy, the next step simply waits.

03Who does what

We take the follow-through.
You keep the decisions.

Every workflow has an authority line, agreed in writing before anything runs. Below it, the agent acts. Above it, the work stops and comes to the right person on your team.

Authority line

Twinthos carries

  • Requesting missing information
  • Recording replies in the customer record
  • Sending the agreed follow-ups on time
  • Assigning the next action to the right person
  • Keeping an account of what was handled

Your team decides

  • Preparing and pricing quotes
  • Discounts beyond the agreed limit
  • Complaints, disputes and anything unusual
  • Changes to the rules the agent follows
  • Access: what it may use, and when that ends
Your part

You provide access, confirm the rules and make the decisions reserved for your team. We handle the agreed setup and the ongoing management.

08Buyer questions

Questions worth answering
before you decide.

Why would we pay monthly?

Because the responsibility continues after launch: monitoring the work, maintaining connections, handling issues and making agreed changes. Your proposal defines that ongoing scope.

Will this give my team more to manage?

Your team helps define the process, grants approved access and handles reserved decisions. We manage the implementation and operation, and we count any remaining review work when judging the value.

We already have AI tools. What would Twinthos add?

We look at what still needs someone to carry information or actions between tools. If your setup already completes the process, another service may add little, and we’ll say so.

Can you guarantee more sales or savings?

No. Sales depend on demand, pricing and delivery as well as follow-up. We define the work Twinthos performs and how its performance will be assessed, and any financial case states its assumptions.

What happens when the agent needs help?

The agreed process sets what requires review, who receives it and how it’s handled. We test the relevant exception cases before activation.

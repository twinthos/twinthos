FAQ

The questions buyers actually ask.

Is this actually replacing people?

No. Twinthos gives repetitive operational work continuous coverage. Your people keep the judgement calls, relationships and decisions. Most clients use recovered capacity to grow rather than cut.

What happens if it makes a mistake?

Important actions run through approval workflows. Agents operate within the rules you configure, flag anything unusual and escalate rather than guessing. Every action is recorded so you can review and tighten the rules.

Will it sound like us?

Yes - that is part of setup. We map your tone, phrases and how you talk to customers. Agents write in your voice, and you review output during the trial week.

What systems can it connect to?

Email, calendars, documents, CRMs and the tools your business already runs on. If your team uses it in a browser, Twinthos can usually work with it. You don't replace your existing software.

Can a human approve important actions?

Yes. You define what needs approval - payments, refunds, sensitive replies. Those actions wait for you. Routine work flows through.

What happens to my data?

Your business runs in an isolated, UK-hosted environment. Your data, your rules, your business. Nothing is shared between clients.

How quickly can we go live?

14 days from kickoff. Day 1-2 is a two-hour mapping call and controlled first tasks; by Day 7 you review real workflow output in the live trial.

Can we stop after the trial?

Yes. If it doesn't work in the first 7 days, you pay £0 and walk away. No lock-in beyond the month you have paid for.

Why does it cost £5,000?

You are buying continuous operational coverage - six specialist agents, 168 hours a week, managed and supervised. The real question is what missed enquiries, late invoices and admin hours already cost you.

What kind of business gets the most value?

UK SMEs with steady enquiry flow and repetitive operational work - estate agencies, law firms, recruiters, accountants, trades and clinics.

Is this suitable for a 10-person company?

Often the smaller the team, the sharper the pain - there is no spare capacity to absorb admin. A 10-person company with constant enquiries is exactly where six always-on agents change the week.

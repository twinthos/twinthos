## The Honest Assessment

Most "AI jobs no experience" lists are SEO filler. This list focuses on **platforms you can actually join today** and **real job postings with working links**. The best entry points are AI training platforms — they accept people from many countries, take no experience, and pay real money. Traditional "junior AI engineer" jobs are much harder to get (most want 1-2 years experience or a degree).

**Tiers, from easiest to hardest:**

1. **AI training platforms** (sign up, take assessment, start getting tasks) — easiest, global
2. **Freelance AI-adjacent work** (Upwork, Contra, Fiverr) — need a skill to sell
3. **Junior AI engineer jobs** (Wellfound, LinkedIn) — need some experience or portfolio

## TIER 1: AI Training Platforms (Sign Up Today, No Experience)

These are the most accessible. You sign up, pass an assessment, and get tasks. Pay varies by platform and your skill.

### DataAnnotation.tech (owned by Surge AI)

**What:** AI training — evaluate AI outputs, write prompts, code review, STEM evaluation.

**Pay:** $20-30+/hr generalist; $50-100+/hr for coding/STEM/professional tracks. One of the better-paying platforms.

**Countries:** US, Canada, UK, Ireland, New Zealand, Australia (6 countries for direct hire). Claims 50+ countries on About page but the job board says 6. **If you're outside these 6, you cannot work directly.**

**Application:** One-time Starter Assessment (unpaid, ~1 hour, ONE attempt only — no retakes). If you pass, you get access to projects. Payment via PayPal, weekly.

**Real pay track record:** $20M+ paid since 2020. 3.7/5 Indeed (700+ reviews), 3.9/5 Glassdoor (300+ reviews).

**Apply:** https://dataannotation.tech/

**FAQ:** https://dataannotation.tech/faqs

**Review:** https://theairankings.com/ai-jobs/dataannotation-review

**NOTE:** Work availability is the big complaint in 2026 — dry spells between tasks. Treat as variable supplemental income.

**What:** AI training — rate and rank LLM outputs, write prompts, create rubrics. Generalist and expert tiers.

**Pay:** Up to $15/hr generalist (English writing/content reviewing). Expert tier higher — up to $150/hr for ML/STEM experts. Pay set per project.

**Countries:** 61 countries (per company reporting). Highest acceptance for US, UK, Canada, Australia, etc. **NOT available in much of Africa.** Must be authorized to work in your country.

**Application:** Sign up on outlier.ai. Assessment-based. No minimum hours.

**Track record:** $100M+ paid to 40,000+ contributors. 4.1/5 on Trustpilot (3k+ reviews).

**Apply:** https://my.oneforma.com/Account/register

## The Reality Check

**Most accessible, decent pay, realistic:**
1. DataAnnotation.tech — IF you're in US/CA/UK/IE/NZ/AU. Best pay-to-effort ratio.
2. Outlier AI — 61 countries, $15/hr generalist, higher for experts. Sign up today.
3. Remotasks — 90+ countries, lower pay but most accessible globally.
4. OneForma — 100+ countries, slow approval, low pay ($7-15/hr), but truly global.

**Higher pay, harder to get:**
5. Mercor — $25-250/hr but needs real domain expertise.
6. OpenTrain AI — Some roles pay $30-80/hr but competitive.
7. Scale AI / Surge AI direct — Hard to get into without connections.

**Traditional junior AI jobs (Wellfound, LinkedIn, Glassdoor):**
- Most want 1-2 years experience
- Remote entry-level is rare — AI Hiring Board says only 1 of 50 entry-level roles was fully remote
- New-grad Forward Deployed Engineer roles pay best ($120K-260K) but are highly competitive

**The smart play:** Start with an AI training platform (Tier 1) to get experience and income, then build toward a junior AI role or expertise that unlocks higher-paying work (Mercor, Scale, etc.).

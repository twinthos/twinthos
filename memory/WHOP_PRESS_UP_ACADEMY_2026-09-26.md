## HOW IT WORKS (Day-to-Day)

### Daily Routine
1. Member does their press-ups (any variation, any number)
2. Member logs their reps in the community (Discord/WhatsApp/Whop chat)
3. Format: "Day 12: 25 press-ups. Knee variation. Feel good."
4. Group reacts, cheers, holds accountable
5. Streak counter updates automatically

### Accountability Mechanics
- **Daily check-in requirement** — Miss a day = streak breaks = public (gentle) accountability
- **Streak tracking** — Visual streak counter. People don't want to break streaks.
- **Leaderboards** — Most reps today. Longest streak. Most improved. Weekly/monthly.
- **Challenges** — "7-day streak challenge." "100 press-up challenge." "Age group challenges."
- **Gentle shame** — "Where's @john today?" — light, friendly accountability
- **Celebration** — Big milestones celebrated publicly (100-day streak, 10,000 total reps, etc.)

### Age-Adaptive Press-Up Tiers
Not everyone can do 50 full press-ups. The academy accommodates all levels:

| Tier | Description | Example Reps |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| Wall press-ups | Standing against wall, easiest | 10-50 reps |
| Knee press-ups | On knees, intermediate | 5-30 reps |
| Full press-ups | Standard | 5-50+ reps |
| Decline/weighted | Hardest variation | 1-20 reps |
| Seniors / mobility-limited | Chair-assisted, partial range | 1-10 reps |

**Key:** Everyone does the same thing (press-ups), at their own level. No one is excluded.

3. **Visible progress** — Reps going up. Streak growing. Body changing. People see results.

8. **Results** — Eventually people see physical changes. That's the ultimate retention.

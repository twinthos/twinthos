**What:** Gate a Discord-style community behind a monthly subscription. Trading groups, sports picks, fitness, masterminds, niches — anything.

**Average earnings:** $4,463/month (across 6,021 earning products)

**How to start:**
1. Go to whop.com and sign up (free, no credit card)
2. Create a Whop
3. Add community features (chat, forums, livestreams)
4. Set monthly price
5. Publish and share your link

**Time to start:** ~5 minutes to set up pricing and publish

**Best niches:** Trading, sports betting, fitness, reselling, crypto, AI, marketing

### METHOD 4: Affiliate Marketing (No Product Needed)

**What:** Promote other people's Whop products and earn commission on every sale.

**Commission:** 30% by default (creators can change this). Recurring on subscriptions.

**How it works:**
1. Create a free Whop account
2. Go to Affiliates dashboard
3. Browse the Affiliate Marketplace — filter by industry, commission rate, earnings, conversion rate
4. Click "Become affiliate" on any product
5. Get your unique referral link
6. Share it anywhere (social media, blog, DMs, YouTube, etc.)

**Two ways to earn as affiliate:**
- **Refer buyers** — 30% commission (default) on every purchase through your link
- **Refer sellers (Whop Partners)** — 25% of Whop's gross profit on transactions

**Best high-paying affiliate opportunities (data from Whop Trends):**
- Bravo Six Picks: $551K/mo revenue, 50% commission
- AI Arbitrage Blueprint: $569K/mo revenue, 30% recurring
- TTW Inner Circle: $678K/mo revenue, 30% recurring

**Pro tip:** Build a review site, YouTube channel, or social media presence around Whop products. With recurring commissions, one referral pays you every month.

**Links:**

- Affiliate docs: https://docs.whop.com/affiliates/promote-your-business/

### METHOD 5: Whop Partners — Refer Businesses

**What:** Refer businesses to join Whop as sellers and earn up to 30% of Whop's gross profit from them.

**Commission:** Up to 30% of Whop's gross profit per referred business, recurring for the business's lifetime. Plus 1% of their ad spend if they run Whop Ads. Plus second-tier commissions when partners you refer bring in businesses.

**Who can do this:** Anyone worldwide. You don't need to be a founder. Just make introductions.

**Who to refer:** Any business that could use better payments infrastructure, storefront hosting, or ad campaigns. Coaches, software companies, agencies — any business model.

**How to start:**
1. Join Whop Partners from your dashboard
2. Create a referral with the business name, website, estimated revenue
3. Whop gives you a referral link and email invite
4. Send it to the business — the sale is yours to close
5. Once they sign up and start selling, you earn

**Earnings potential:** Top partners clear 6 figures per referral.

**Links:** https://whop.com/blog/whop-partners/

### METHOD 6: Create and Publish Blueprints

**What:** Build a complete Whop business template (blueprint) and publish it. When someone deploys your blueprint, you earn 10% of Whop's profit on every sale that business makes.

**Commission:** 10% of Whop's profit on sales through every deployed copy of your blueprint. Attributed automatically. Comes from Whop's share, not the seller's — so it doesn't affect their pricing or payouts.

**What's in a blueprint:** A complete business — every page, every product, every price, wired to a working checkout, deployed on a subdomain.

**Examples of blueprints:** Salon, gym, jeweler, martial arts academy, safari outfitter, high-ticket coaching funnel, creative agency, neobank.

**How to create:**
1. Build a business on Whop (manually or via AI chat)
2. Go to Websites > publish as blueprint
3. Or use the CLI: clone a blueprint source, customize, redeploy

**Pro tip:** One blueprint per business. The blueprint is the distribution unit — when someone clicks "Use this blueprint," they get the entire business, not just a design theme.

**Links:**
- Blueprints blog: https://whop.com/blog/whop-blueprints/
- Blueprint docs: https://docs.whop.com/developer/websites/blueprints/
- Blueprint gallery: https://whop.com/blueprints/

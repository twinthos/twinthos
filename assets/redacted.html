This is a real operations audit we ran. The client name and exact figures are removed. The structure, the findings and the recommended handover are intact.

01 Workflow identified

Enquiry-to-appointment process: a recurring operational sequence touching three systems, handled by two people across four steps.

02 Current manual process

Someone checks for new enquiries, reads the details, copies information across systems, confirms the appointment, sends the reminder, updates the record. Six movements for one result.

03 Recommended handover

The enquiry-to-appointment sequence, with agreed rules for what Twinthos can do alone and what requires a person.

04 Human boundary

Twinthos handles the check, the copy, the update and the reminder. A person approves any appointment above the agreed value and any exception to the standard process.

05 Estimated value

Based on the time usually spent on this workflow and the number of times it runs each week. The actual number is confirmed during the audit.

06 Implementation plan

One workflow, one human boundary, a short setup window, then monitored operation. The plan specifies what is handed over first and what is left for later.

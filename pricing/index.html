What's included in the managed service
Infrastructure

The systems the workflow runs on, monitoring, access control and credential management — configured and maintained as part of the service.

Deployment

The workflow runs across the tools you already use. The repeatable parts are assumed by the agent; the rest stays with your team.

Monitoring

Nothing disappears without being measured first. You see what the workflow handled, where it paused, and what needs your attention.

Maintenance

Changes to connected tools, rule updates, fixes when something breaks. We handle it; you don't maintain infrastructure.

Exception handling

Anything outside the agreed rules pauses and comes to a person. No action without agreed authority. No surprises.

Reporting

Regular, readable summary of what the workflow handled. No filler. No noise.

Support

Direct contact with the person who designed and runs your workflows. Not a ticketing system. Not a team you route through.

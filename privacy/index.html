Privacy

How we handle information.

Plain language. No filler. What we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how to exercise your rights.

What we collect

Only what the service needs.

To provide the service

When you book a fit call or request an audit, we collect the information you provide — name, email, company, and the details of the work you describe. We use it to respond to you and, if you proceed, to set up the workflow.

To run a workflow

A workflow accesses the information it needs to do the work — emails, calendar entries, CRM records, invoices, or whatever the specific task requires. We document exactly what each workflow accesses before it's activated.

To communicate

Your email address, to follow up on your request, confirm calls, and send updates about the service. We don't send marketing emails. If you want us to stop, tell us and we will.

Technical records

Activity logs for the workflows we run — what the agent did, when, and what it paused for. These exist so you can see what happened and we can maintain the service. Retention periods are documented per client.

Cookies

We don't run a tracking stack.

This site doesn't use cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising. The booking form uses a session to keep your submission on the page — that's it. We don't share data with third-party analytics providers.

How long we keep it

Not forever. Not arbitrarily.

Fit call and audit request details are kept for as long as needed to provide the service and for a reasonable period after in case you return. Workflow activity logs are retained per the documented period for your specific setup. We don't hold data longer than the service requires.

To delete your data

Email hello@twinthos.com and we'll tell you what we hold and delete it. For workflow data, deletion is part of the service-end process documented per client.

Your rights

You can ask, and we'll respond.

Access

You can ask what personal data we hold about you and receive a copy.

Correction

If something is wrong, tell us and we'll correct it.

Deletion

You can ask us to delete your personal data, subject to any legal or contractual obligations that require us to retain it.

Opt out

You can stop the service at any time. The managed service ends. Any data covered by the service end process is handled as documented for your setup.

Questions or requests

Email hello@twinthos.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time and in plain language.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted at the top of this page. The current version is always at twinthos.com/privacy/.

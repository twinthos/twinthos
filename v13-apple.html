Questions

Asked, answered.

Is Twinthos a chatbot?+
No. A chatbot answers one message and forgets you. Twinthos is a team of workers that owns a whole job - it answers, chases, books, files and follows up, remembers everything, and works around the clock.
What does it cost?+
£5,000 a month, flat. The whole team, setup, UK hosting, human supervision and a weekly report. No setup fees, no usage limits.
How long until it is live?+
14 days. Week one we learn your business. Week two we build your team and you watch them with training wheels. Then a human supervises around the clock.
Will it sound like a robot?+
No. Twinthos learns your tone from your own files and replies to routine messages in your voice. You approve anything important before it goes out.
What if it makes a mistake?+
A human supervises every worker. Anything uncertain is flagged to you before it goes out. And if it is not working for you in the first 7 days, you do not pay.
Is my data safe?+
Yes. Your data stays in the UK, is never used to train AI, and each client runs in their own isolated environment.

02 What we find

What the bottlenecks look like.

These are real examples from real audits. The numbers are illustrative - yours will be different. The pattern is the same: owner is the bottleneck, work is being done by a person that does not need a person, money is being left on the table by a workflow that is half-built.

01

Enquiries answered late, or not at all

A lead fills the website form at 10pm. The owner sees the email at 8am the next morning. The lead has called two competitors by then. Sector data: 78% of buyers hire the first responder (MIT, InsideSales). Lost revenue per missed after-hours enquiry for a £5k service: roughly £1,200 per incident.
~£4-8k/mo
02

Quote follow-up done by hand, slipping through the cracks

Three quotes go out Monday. By Friday two have not been chased. The owner remembers on the weekend. By Monday the buyer has bought elsewhere. Conversion lift from a structured 5-touch follow-up sequence: 30-50% on warm quotes.
~£3-6k/mo
03

Booking back-and-forth eating 5-8 hours of owner time a week

Email tennis. What time works for you. How about Tuesday. Actually Thursday. Owner has 11 of these conversations a day. Owner time is the most expensive time in the business. At £80/hr loaded cost, that is £400-£640/week of owner time spent on calendar admin.
~£1.7-2.5k/mo
04

Customer support questions going to the owner

"Where is my order." "How do I reset my password." "Can you send me the invoice again." Twelve emails a day to the owner. Each one a 6-minute interruption that costs 25 minutes of focus recovery on the actual revenue work.
~£1.5-2k/mo
05

Reporting and admin eating Friday afternoon

Spreadsheet updates. CRM cleanup. Invoice reconciliation. The owner does it because nobody else knows how. Every Friday, 4-6 hours. That is 20% of the owner's productive week gone to work that does not need a person.
~£1.5-2k/mo

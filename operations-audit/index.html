Operations audit

A practical plan for the work your business could hand over.

The Twinthos operations audit maps your recurring work, identifies the most useful starting points and sets out what deployment would involve.

What you receive

Six things. The fifth one is the plan.

Deliverable 01

Executive summary

The opportunity in plain terms — what's repeating, what it's costing in attention, and where the most useful starting point is.

Deliverable 02

Workflow and dependency map

Your recurring work and the systems it depends on — what arrives, what the outcome should be, what tools are involved.

Deliverable 03

Priorities ranked by impact and effort

Where to start and why. Not every opportunity is equally useful — we rank them by what's achievable and what moves the needle.

Deliverable 04

Business-case assumptions and unknowns

What the numbers rest on, and what must be measured. No invented savings figures. We separate what we know from what would need to be tracked.

Deliverable 05

Starting scope and team involvement

The first workflow we'd recommend, what it would handle, what it wouldn't, and where your team stays involved. Written before anything is deployed.

Deliverable 06

Practical implementation plan

Steps, access and timing. What we'd need, what we'd do, and roughly when. Enough to act on, not a vague roadmap.

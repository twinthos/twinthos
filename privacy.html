What we hold

Your working data, not marketing data

The files and instructions you give us so your workers can do their jobs, the messages they send and receive on your behalf, and the operational logs that show what they did.

This is data you own. It is not used for anything outside your service.

Where it lives

UK-hosted. Isolated per client.

UK-hosted infrastructure. Each client runs in an isolated environment: your workers, your machine boundary, your data. Nothing you give Twinthos is shared with other clients.

What never happens

Never used for advertising

Twinthos · Privacy

Your data, in your hands.

The short version

Twinthos is operated by Tyson Architect, Bath, United Kingdom. Your business data stays in the UK, is never used to train AI models, and you can export or delete it on request. We only keep clients who would choose us over a junior hire, and that starts with trust in how we handle information.

What we hold

The files and instructions you give us so your workers can do their jobs, the messages they send and receive on your behalf, and the operational logs that show what they did. This is working data you own, not marketing data we mine.

Where it lives

UK-hosted infrastructure. Each client runs in an isolated environment: your workers, your machine boundary, your data. Nothing you give Twinthos is shared with other clients.

What never happens

Your data is never sold, never used to train AI models, and never used for advertising. A human in the UK supervises your workers, and anything uncertain is flagged to you before it goes out.

Contact and requests

Export or deletion requests, and any privacy questions: hello@twinthos.com. We answer every request personally.

Case studies

Real work, handed over.

Three businesses. One repeated task each. What Twinthos took on, and what the team got back.

Agency delivery coordination

Sarah Chen, Operations Director, Meridian Retail Group

The bottleneck. Every client brief arrived in a different format, feedback was scattered across email, Slack and WhatsApp, and someone spent two hours a day turning conversations into tasks and status updates.

What Twinthos took on. Collecting incoming requests, retrieving project context from the agreed folder structure, asking for missing details, and preparing a structured task list with the next action and owner.

What the team got back. Ten hours a week of coordination work. The account team spent more time on the work clients pay for and less time chasing formats and chasing approvals.

Result: 10 hours recovered a week. Brief-to-task time fell from 2 hours to 20 minutes.

Property viewing coordination

James O'Brien, Finance Manager, Harbour Point Properties

The bottleneck. Viewing requests, availability and property details sat in three places. Staff repeated the same conversation with every applicant and still missed follow-ups.

What Twinthos took on. Collecting enquiry details, retrieving approved property information from the CRM, coordinating available viewing slots, updating the record, and sending confirmation texts.

What the team got back. Fewer conversations left waiting. Less back-and-forth for the team. Applicant selection, referencing and tenancy decisions stayed with the team.

Result: 8 hours recovered a week. Viewing bookings became automatic.

Recruitment interview scheduling

Priya Sharma, Managing Director, Elevate Recruitment

The bottleneck. Coordinating interview slots between candidates and three interviewers required repeated calendar checks, reminder texts and rescheduling emails.

What Twinthos took on. Collecting candidate availability, checking interviewer calendars against the agreed rules, sending confirmed interview invites, and recording the outcome in the ATS.

What the team got back. Less diary coordination. More time for recruiter conversations. Shortlisting, ranking and hiring decisions stayed with the people.

Result: 12 hours recovered a week. No-show rate dropped from 18% to 4%.

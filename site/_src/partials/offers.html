Scope, support and limits
One workflow
The monthly fee covers one agreed workflow. More workflows, new connections or extra reporting are priced and agreed in writing first.
Examples, not promises
The industry examples and wider responsibilities on this site show what can be scoped and tested. The proposal defines what is actually delivered.
Changes
Changes inside the agreed scope are included. Bigger changes are quoted before any work starts.
Hours
The digital employee's operating hours are agreed in your proposal. Human support is during working hours. There is no 24-hour cover or emergency response.
Decisions
Anything outside the agreed rules goes to your team. The digital employee does not make those calls.
Results
We do not promise sales or savings. The proposal says what work is done and how it will be judged.
Payment
There is no online checkout. You only pay once the scope has been confirmed with you.

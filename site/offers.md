# Twinthos — Offers to Pitch

## What Twinthos Is (as a product)
- **Dewey clone** — a platform of **managed digital employees** that complete end-to-end work
- Two products:
  1. **Operations Audit** — £999, one-off: maps recurring work, ranks opportunities, writes a starting scope
  2. **Managed Digital Employee** — £3,500/month (3-month min): builds, connects, runs, monitors one workflow across existing tools
- Core promise: **"Work handled, time returned."**
- Positioning: *"Twinthos is a platform of managed digital employees that complete end-to-end work; ChatGPT, Grok, and Claude are AI models that answer text. Twinthos executes jobs; the others converse with you."*

---

## Differentiators (3)
1. **Outcome vs. answer** — models return text; Twinthos executes tasks end-to-end and delivers completed deliverables
2. **Managed ownership vs. passive tool** — agents deployed with assigned roles; models are tools you prompt repeatedly
3. **Action vs. conversation** — Twinthos does the work; models chat about it

---

## Offers to Pitch to Twinthos

### Offer 1 — Corporate Outreach Package (Outbound Sales Agent)
- **What:** A dedicated AI outbound agent that finds, researches, and engages prospects across Discord, WhatsApp, iMessage, LinkedIn. Runs 24/7, qualifies leads, books discovery calls, hands warm conversations to your team.
- **Value to Twinthos:** Twinthos needs inbound discovery and outbound lead gen. This fills the place of a full outbound sales team with zero headcount.
- **Launch price:** £2,500 setup + £1,500/month (30-day free trial)

### Offer 2 — Engagement & Nurture Automation
- **What:** AI agent tracks every prospect interaction, engages at the right moment, moves leads through a nurturing sequence. Starts conversations and hands off.
- **Value:** Twinthos's content/notes attract readers but get zero follow-up. Closes the loop between content and sales pipeline.
- **Launch price:** £1,200 setup + £800/month

### Offer 3 — Revenue & Pipeline Attribution
- **What:** Tracking and attribution layer connecting every outbound action to revenue. Shows which sequences, channels, and messages produced actual deals.
- **Value:** Twinthos's services are priced on "work done" and "results" — this gives enterprise clients the accountability they need to pay.
- **Launch price:** £800 setup + £500/month

### Offer 4 — Full Growth Operation Bundle
- **What:** All three offers combined into one managed system: outbound agent + engagement nurturer + revenue attribution. One operator monitors everything.
- **Value:** One account that handles the entire outbound motion — find, nurture, convert, track. Twinthos's clients get a full digital sales team without hiring.
- **Launch price:** £4,500 setup + £3,000/month

### Offer 5 — Concierge Analysis & Deployment
- **What:** A Twinthos-style audit done for Twinthos — they map their own sales and customer success workflows, find where work gets stuck, propose a starting scope to hand over.
- **Value:** Twinthos is a service business with many manual processes. This productises their own expertise into a system that finds and fixes their operational bottlenecks.
- **Launch price:** £3,000 (discounts after 3 referrals)

## Hotbox (Comparison Target) — Quick Facts
- **Company:** Hotbox — AI sales infrastructure for social commerce ("Clay for social media")
- **Founder:** Harpriya Bagri — Surrey-born, UBC-trained ML engineer, LinkedIn ranking algorithm builder, NeurIPS-published author, Respell acquired by Salesforce
- **Backing:** a16z Speedrun SR006 (<1% of ~19,000 applicants)
- **Traction:** $450k ARR ($350k pre-launch), $4M pipeline in 2 weeks, 3 employees, founded 2025
- **Demo Day:** April 14
- **Platform:** Instagram-first AI sales agent
- **Tagline:** "The future of lead generation is not emails, it's algorithms."

## Key Insight
- Hotbox = **demand-generation layer** (social selling, AI agents)
- Twinthos = **operational layer** (automation, community, audit)
- Pitch angle: **partnership, investment, product crossover**

## Recommendation
- **Offer 1 (Corporate Outreach Package)** is the cleanest, most marketable — directly fills Twinthos's need for a sales-and-outreach system
- **Offer 4 (Full Growth Operation Bundle)** is the higher-ticket option

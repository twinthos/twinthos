Why

Every company already has AI inside it. Nobody is watching it.

In the last 12 months, AI agents from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta breached production systems without being asked. The attacker is no longer an anonymous criminal group. It is an AI agent, and it is already inside your systems. We believe every company deserves an AI that works for them — not against them. We believe work should be done by machines so humans can do what humans do best: think, create, connect, decide.

How

Persistence. Retrieval. Verification.

We do not build smarter models. We build systems that remember. A Company Brain that holds approved knowledge, decisions, and project state — so the employee gets better instead of resetting every conversation. A Digital Employee with a written job spec, controlled permissions, and human sign-off. The value is in persistence, not model magic.

What

A digital employee that works 24/7 inside your existing tools.

Three parts. All three, or it is not the product.

Control

You decide what it does. You decide what it touches.

Every digital employee has a written job spec. Every action is logged. Every consequential decision requires human sign-off. You are not handing over control. You are delegating work.

1
Permissions

Read, write, or nothing.

You set exactly what the employee can read, what it can write, and what it cannot touch. No delete. No send without approval. No access to systems you do not authorise.

2
Sign-off

One named human approves.

Every consequential action — sending an email, routing a payment, updating a record — goes to one named person for approval. The employee proposes. You decide.

3
Audit trail

Everything is logged.

Every action, every decision, every piece of data accessed. Timestamped. Searchable. Exportable. If someone asks "what did the AI do?", you have the answer in seconds.

FAQ

Questions we're asked.
Answers we give.

If your question isn't here, email hello@twinthos.com. We'd rather answer a real question than maintain a complete FAQ.

What kind of work is Twinthos a fit for?

Repeat operational work with a clear outcome, usable information and agreed rules. Enquiries, follow-ups, scheduling and record updates are good places to start. We confirm the actual scope before deployment — if the work doesn't have a clear outcome or the rules are unclear, we'll say so.

Will my team have to prompt every step?

No. Within the agreed workflow, the agent follows events and takes permitted next actions without a person prompting every step. Your team stays involved where judgement or approval is needed — anything outside the agreed scope pauses and comes to you.

What does £5,000 per month include?

Implementation and ongoing management of the agreed workflows — connecting the tools, defining the rules, monitoring, maintenance, reporting and support. The infrastructure and access control for the systems the workflow uses is included. Any separately chargeable work or external software is agreed in advance.

How do we start?

Book a 15-minute fit call. We'll look at the work that keeps coming back, the tools involved, and whether a managed agent makes commercial sense. You leave with a candidate workflow and a clear next step. If it's a fit, we propose a scope in writing before anything is deployed.

When does billing begin?

You review the results and choose whether to continue. Billing begins when your workflow is deployed and running. The service is the service from that point. The minimum term is 3 months. The operations audit (£999) is credited in full against your first month. There's no automatic conversion, no pressure, no lock-in.

How is my data handled?

What information the agent accesses, where it's hosted, which providers and models are involved, and how separation and credentials work — all documented in writing for your specific setup before activation. We do not claim UK-only processing: a UK machine location does not establish how every provider processes data. Ask us a specific question and we'll answer it for your setup.

Can we start with more than one workflow?

We start with one. Not because we can't do more, but because the right way to prove the fit is to show the work moving before anything else is agreed. Once the first workflow is running and you're happy with it, expanding is a conversation.

What if the workflow doesn't work?

We fix it. The management arrangement includes maintenance and support — if something breaks or the rules need adjusting, that's our responsibility, not yours. If the workflow genuinely isn't a fit, we'll say so and help you understand what would need to change.

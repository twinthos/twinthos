Questions

Straight answers before you decide.

If your situation is different, bring it to the fit call and we will be specific.

New to AI? Three short explanations

AI: software that can work with information and follow instructions. Digital employee: AI software set up for one agreed business job, not a person. Operations audit: a paid review that ends with a written plan, not a working system.

Is a digital employee a real person?

No. It's AI software set up to handle an agreed job such as replying to messages or updating records. It works within rules you approve, and asks a person when something needs judgement.

Will it replace my staff?

No. It takes the repeat follow-through off them: the chasing, the updating, the reminding. Decisions, relationships and judgement stay with your people.

Why is the managed service monthly?

Because the work continues after launch. We keep tools connected, fix problems and make agreed changes. Your written offer says what is included.

What's the difference between the £999 review and monthly service?

The £999 review gives you a written plan. It does not set anything up. The £5,000 monthly service sets up and runs one agreed job.

Do we need the audit first?

No. It is optional. Some businesses start with a discovery call and go straight to a proposal for the managed service.

What does £5,000 per month cover?

We build and test one agreed job, connect the tools it needs, check that it keeps working and make agreed changes. Your written offer tells you exactly what is included and what costs extra.

What is the minimum term?

Three months, so £15,000 in service fees. Billing starts when the agreed job is running. After that, either side can end it with 30 days’ written notice. Prices exclude VAT where applicable.

Is the audit fee credited?

If you go ahead with the managed service within 30 days of receiving the audit, the £999 is credited in full against your first month.

Will this give my team more to manage?

Your team helps define the process, gives the agreed access and makes the decisions above the line. We build and run the rest. Any review work left for your team is counted when we judge whether it is worth it.

We already use AI tools. What would you add?

We look for the places where someone still carries information from one tool to another. If your setup already does the whole job, we may add little, and we will say so.

Can you promise more sales or savings?

No. Sales depend on demand, pricing and delivery as well as follow-up. We agree what work Twinthos does and how it will be judged. Any money figures show their assumptions.

What happens if the software is unsure?

We agree which decisions need a person and how to ask them. We test unusual situations before work starts.

How is our information handled?

We explain which accounts it needs, which companies may handle your information, and how your information is used before the job starts. See Security and data.

Who runs the service, and when?

One person responsible designs and runs the workflow and deals with problems. Human support is during working hours. It is not a 24-hour service.

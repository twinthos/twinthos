Questions

Questions worth answering before you decide.

If something here doesn’t match your situation, raise it on the fit call and we’ll be specific.

Why would we pay monthly?

Because the responsibility continues after launch: monitoring the work, maintaining connections, handling issues and making agreed changes. Your proposal defines that ongoing scope.

Will this give my team more to manage?

Your team helps define the process, grants approved access and handles reserved decisions. We manage the implementation and operation, and we count any remaining review work when judging the value.

We already have AI tools. What would Twinthos add?

We look at what still needs someone to carry information or actions between tools. If your setup already completes the process, another service may add little, and we’ll say so.

Can you guarantee more sales or savings?

No. Sales depend on demand, pricing and delivery as well as follow-up. We define the work Twinthos performs and how its performance will be assessed, and any financial case states its assumptions.

What happens when the agent needs help?

The agreed process sets what requires review, who receives it and how it’s handled. We test the relevant exception cases before activation.

How is our information handled?

We document the access, providers and data arrangements for your workflow in writing before activation. See Security and data.

What does the £5,000/month cover?

The agreed managed service: workflow design and implementation, connections to agreed systems, testing, monitoring and maintenance, agreed rule changes, and operational reporting and support. Your proposal defines the specific scope and any separately charged software.

What is the minimum term?

Three months. Billing starts when your workflow is live. After the minimum term, either side may end the service with 30 days’ written notice. Prices exclude VAT where applicable.

What is the operations audit?

A £999 written plan that maps where recurring work gets stuck and what a first deployment would involve. You keep it either way. The fee is credited in full against your first managed-service month if you proceed within 30 days of receiving the audit.

Who runs the service?

A named operator designs and manages the workflows and handles service issues directly. Support covers the agreed scope during working hours; it is not a 24-hour service.

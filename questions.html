These are the questions we hear most often before the assessment. Read them here, or bring your own to the call — that is what the 15 minutes is for.

What can Twinthos actually take on?

Start with recurring work that has a clear outcome, usable information and agreed rules. Enquiries, follow-ups, scheduling and record updates are good workflows to assess. We confirm the actual scope before deployment.

Will my team need to prompt it?

Within the agreed workflow, the agent can follow events and take permitted next actions without a person prompting every step. Your team remains involved where the workflow requires judgement or approval.

What happens when it gets stuck?

The workflow pauses or escalates when it cannot continue safely within its instructions. The managed service includes issue handling and a review process with defined contacts, support hours and response expectations.

Can important actions require approval?

Yes. Approval requirements are part of the agreed workflow. Identify the actions, spending limits and exceptions that must wait for a person before activation. The system only acts within its documented authority.

Can Twinthos use our existing software?

We assess the systems your workflow depends on and confirm supported actions before the pilot. Compatibility depends on access, integration support and the reliability of the specific workflow. We only connect what the workflow actually needs.

How is business data handled?

Before activation, we document the information the workflow needs, the systems that process it and the access granted. Data stays UK-hosted, is never used to train AI, and is never sold or used for advertising. Export and deletion are available on request. See the privacy page for the full position.

Still have a question?

The 15-minute assessment call is for the questions that are specific to your operation. Bring whatever you like.

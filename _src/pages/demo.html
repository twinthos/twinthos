What happened

One enquiry. Four steps.
A clear next step.

01 · Mon 09:14

Enquiry received

The enquiry is logged against the customer and checked for what a quote needs.

02 · Mon 09:15

Information requested

The agent asks for the site postcode, number of units and preferred timing, in wording you approved.

03 · Mon 11:03

Recorded and assigned

The reply is recorded and the enquiry is assigned for quotation. Preparing the quote stays with your team.

04 · Thu 10:00

Follow-up sent

When the agreed follow-up becomes due, it is sent, any reply is recorded and the next action is logged.

The approval that stays with you

If the customer asks to reduce the quote by more than 10%, the agent does not change it. The request and its context go to your team to decide.

This is an illustrative example using sample data. It explains how the service works. It is not a customer result, and no real messages or customer information are used.

03Who does what

We take the follow-through.
You keep the decisions.

Every workflow has an authority line, agreed in writing before anything runs. Below it, the agent acts. Above it, the work stops and comes to the right person on your team.

Authority line

Twinthos carries

  • Requesting missing information
  • Recording replies in the customer record
  • Sending the agreed follow-ups on time
  • Assigning the next action to the right person
  • Keeping an account of what was handled

Your team decides

  • Preparing and pricing quotes
  • Discounts beyond the agreed limit
  • Complaints, disputes and anything unusual
  • Changes to the rules the agent follows
  • Access: what it may use, and when that ends
Your part

You provide access, confirm the rules and make the decisions reserved for your team. We handle the agreed setup and the ongoing management.

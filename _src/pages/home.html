07 / Before we begin

The questions
worth asking.

Good automation starts with honest limits, not exaggerated promises.

Is this just ChatGPT connected to software?+

No. We scope an ongoing operational responsibility, configure a dedicated working environment, define allowed actions and establish the rules for exceptions and review. Each implementation depends on the job and available integrations.

Can I control it from a message?+

Messaging interfaces can be part of the agreed deployment. You can issue instructions through connected channels such as Slack or Telegram when set up; access, identity and permissions must be configured first.

Will it run without me watching it?+

Routine steps can run under written rules and an agreed schedule. Sensitive requests, missing information or actions outside those rules are routed to your team. Human support hours and monitoring arrangements are set out in the proposal.

What if the workflow makes a mistake?+

We specify what the agent can do, test representative normal and abnormal cases, and design review and recovery into the workflow. No automated system is risk-free; especially consequential actions should require appropriate oversight.

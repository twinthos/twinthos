Questions people ask first

Will it replace my staff?

No. It takes the repeat follow-through off them: the chasing, the updating, the reminding. Decisions, relationships and judgement stay with your people.

What if it gets something wrong?

It works inside written rules, and anything outside them goes to a person. We test unusual cases before go-live, then watch and fix it as part of the monthly service.

Do we need to change our tools?

No. It works in the tools you already use, once we have agreed access. There is no new dashboard for your team to check.

Is it really an employee?

It is AI software that does a defined job, so we call it a digital employee. It is not a person, it does not make your decisions, and one accountable Twinthos operator is responsible for it.

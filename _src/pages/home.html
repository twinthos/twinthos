02 / A COMPLETE WORKING ENVIRONMENT

Intelligence is not enough.
Give it the means to work.

A digital employee needs tools, context, clear instructions and a boundary. We configure those around one agreed responsibility.

  1. 01

    Its own computer

    A dedicated cloud workspace and browser for permitted applications.

  2. 02

    Its own communications

    An inbox for authorised correspondence. Phone and team-chat channels where configured.

  3. 03

    Your business context

    Approved procedures, documents and task history, with reviewable information boundaries.

  4. 04

    Connections to your tools

    Access limited to the applications and actions the defined role requires.

  5. 05

    Scheduled responsibility

    Tasks can start from an approved trigger or schedule, including outside office hours.

  6. 06

    Human authority

    Activity records, exceptions, approval gates and ongoing oversight by Twinthos.

Capabilities are subject to technical feasibility, permissions, agreed scope and testing. Human support is provided during working hours; this is not a promise of 24-hour staffed support.

    Questions buyers ask first

    Is it really an employee?

    It is software, not a person. We call it a digital employee because it is given a role, works through its own computer, phone and email, and carries out agreed responsibilities. You decide what it may do. Anything outside the agreed rules goes to your team.

    Will it replace my team?

    It takes on defined, repeatable work so your people can spend more of the day on the work only they can do. We do not promise savings or headcount changes.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    Work is tested before launch and monitored while it runs. Your team reviews and approves what needs approval, and we fix and refine the procedure when something is off.

    Why not just use ChatGPT?

    General-purpose AI assistants can research, draft and take some actions. Twinthos is a managed business-specific deployment: we define one job, connect approved tools, agree boundaries, test it and monitor its operation.

    Which tools can it work with?

    The systems and permissions assigned to its role. We confirm what connects, and test it, before anything is promised. A tool being technically connectable does not mean it is connected for you.

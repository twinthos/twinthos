    Questions buyers ask first

    Is it really an employee?

    It is software, not a person. We call it a digital employee because it is given a role, works through its own computer, phone and email, and carries out agreed responsibilities. You decide what it may do. Anything outside the agreed rules goes to your team.

    Will it replace my team?

    It takes on defined, repeatable work so your people can spend more of the day on the work only they can do. We do not promise savings or headcount changes.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    Work is tested before launch and monitored while it runs. Your team reviews and approves what needs approval, and we fix and refine the procedure when something is off.

    Why not just use ChatGPT?

    ChatGPT gives you answers in a chat window. Twinthos gives you a digital employee that does the work: its own computer, inbox and phone, working inside your tools, following your procedures and reporting what it did. We set it up, monitor it and improve it for you.

    Which tools can it work with?

    The systems and permissions assigned to its role. We confirm what connects, and test it, before anything is promised. A tool being technically connectable does not mean it is connected for you.

Digital employeeSet up for one role in your business
At work

  • Its own computer

    A secure cloud desktop with a browser and the apps its role needs. It keeps working when the office is closed.

  • Its own inbox

    Receives requests, replies, chases and files correspondence in your tone of voice.

  • Its own phone number

    Sends and receives texts and calls for confirmations, reminders and follow-ups.

  • In your team chat

    Assign and check work through Slack, Telegram or Microsoft Teams, where configured, just as you would with a colleague.

  • Inside your tools

    Works in the systems you already use, such as Gmail, Outlook, Xero, HubSpot and Google Drive, with only the access you grant.

  • Memory of your business

    Remembers clients, preferences and past work, so nobody explains things twice. You can read and correct it.

  • Your procedures, as skills

    Your documents and call notes become step-by-step skills it follows the same way every time.

  • A card with limits

    Optional. Pays approved costs within limits you set. Anything above them comes to you first.

  • Managed by Twinthos

    Set up, monitored, maintained and improved by the Twinthos team. You never manage the technology.

  • Work that runs on schedule

    Checks queues, prepares reports and follows up on agreed schedules or approved triggers, including outside office hours.

  • A record of the work

    See completed actions, outstanding tasks, exceptions and approvals. Your team can review what happened.

  • Permissions and approvals

    Access is limited to agreed systems and actions. Sensitive decisions, exceptions and spending can be escalated to you.

Every employee is scoped and tested for your systems. Phone features, spending and operating hours depend on the agreed deployment.

    Questions buyers ask first

    Is it really an employee?

    It is software, not a person. We call it a digital employee because it is given a role, works through its own computer, phone and email, and carries out agreed responsibilities. You decide what it may do. Anything outside the agreed rules goes to your team.

    Will it replace my team?

    It takes on defined, repeatable work so your people can spend more of the day on the work only they can do. We do not promise savings or headcount changes.

    What if it gets something wrong?

    Work is tested before launch and monitored while it runs. Your team reviews and approves what needs approval, and we fix and refine the procedure when something is off.

    Why not just use ChatGPT?

    ChatGPT gives you answers in a chat window. Twinthos gives you a digital employee that does the work: its own computer, inbox and phone, working inside your tools, following your procedures and reporting what it did. We set it up, monitor it and improve it for you.

    Which tools can it work with?

    The systems and permissions assigned to its role. We confirm what connects, and test it, before anything is promised. A tool being technically connectable does not mean it is connected for you.
